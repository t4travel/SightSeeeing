Overview

Cairns Aquarium is on three levels in the city centre, with more than 16,000 animals across 10 ecosystems from river to reef. Features include a Deep Reef Tank, a 360-degree Oceanarium and a 20 m underwater tunnel.

Daily talks, ray and shark feeding and a touch tank are included. A 30-minute Turtle Hospital tour can be added. Prices below are reseller listings: Verify.
