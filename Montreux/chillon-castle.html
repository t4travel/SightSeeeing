Overview

Chillon is a castle on a rock in Lake Geneva at Veytaux, a short distance from Montreux, with a history of about a thousand years as a stronghold of the Counts of Savoy and later a Bernese bailiff's seat. Visitors walk through courtyards, halls, vaults, the Savoy chapel and the dungeon, where the prison of François Bonivard inspired Lord Byron's poem.

Eight short films on terminals explain the castle's story. Groups of 15 or more can book a 50-minute guided visit with a guide in medieval costume, or a wine-themed guided tour of about 80 minutes, with 3 working days' notice.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Closed on 1 January and 25 December (one guide listing, Verify).

Hours: Summer: 09:00–19:00, last admission 18:00. Winter: 09:30–18:00, last admission 17:00 (official).

Best time: A weekday morning, with the Fort de Chillon after lunch.

Meeting point & getting there

Avenue de Chillon 21, 1820 Veytaux, about 4 km from Montreux. Bus 201 (stop Chillon), Veytaux-Chillon station (300 m) or boat from Montreux, Villeneuve or Lausanne.

FAQs

Is Chillon free with the Swiss Travel Pass?

Yes, entry is free with the Swiss Travel Pass and Swiss Museum Pass.

How long does it take?

About 2 hours.

How do we get there from Montreux?

Bus 201, rail to Veytaux-Chillon, or a boat.

Is there a guided tour?

Yes for groups of 15+, by reservation; there are also audio guides.
