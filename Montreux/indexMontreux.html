About Montreux and the Swiss Riviera

Montreux sits on the eastern end of Lake Geneva between the Lavaux vineyard terraces and the Rhône valley. The Swiss Riviera is known for Chillon Castle, the lakeside promenade with the Freddie Mercury statue, Chaplin's World in Corsier-sur-Vevey, the UNESCO Lavaux terraces and the cogwheel train to Rochers-de-Naye. Many Indian travellers pass through Montreux only on the GoldenPass line: a night here makes it a destination in its own right.

Getting there and around

  • Rail: Montreux is on the Lausanne – Brig line and the end of the GoldenPass Express from Interlaken (about 3 hr 15). Bus 201 serves Chillon; Veytaux-Chillon station is 300 m from the castle.
  • Passes: Swiss Travel Pass (castle free), Swiss Half Fare Card, Lake Geneva regional offers (Verify).
  • Boats: CGN steamers and Swiss Riviera cruises from the lakeside piers.

Tips for Indian travellers

  • Chaplin's World is a strong draw for film lovers: Chaplin lived at the Manoir de Ban from 1953 to 1977.
  • Plan an overnight so the GoldenPass journey is not rushed: the last return Express from Montreux can be early afternoon (Verify).
  • Switzerland Tourism India webinar (March 2024, Verify for 2026): Swiss Schengen visa applications can be made up to 6 months ahead through VFS Global, with processing of at least 13–15 working days from when documents reach the visa section.
