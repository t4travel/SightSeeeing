Overview

The Colosseum is the largest Roman amphitheatre ever built. The standard ticket also covers the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill within 24 hours.

Each ticket allows a single entry per site.

Opening hours & closed days

The Colosseum opens at 08:30 and the Roman Forum–Palatine area at 09:00. Closing times change with the season: Verify on the official site.

FAQs

Does the ticket cover the Forum and Palatine?

Yes, the standard ticket covers all three within 24 hours.

Is there a different price for Indian passport holders?

No. The price is the same for non-EU visitors.

Can I see the arena floor?

Only with the Full Experience ticket.

How long do I need?

About 2.5 to 3 hours for all three sites.
