Overview

Thorpe Park is a roller-coaster park in Surrey with rides including Stealth, Hyperia, SAW – The Ride and Colossus. The 2026 season runs 27 March to 1 November.

Fright Nights runs from 2 October to 1 November 2026 with extended hours.
