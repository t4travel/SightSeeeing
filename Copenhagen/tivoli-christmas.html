Overview

From 13 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 Tivoli is dressed for Christmas, with lit trees, Christmas stalls, Santa and festive rides, according to Tivoli's own season page.

Think of it as a lit amusement garden with Christmas stalls rather than a large traditional market. Entrance and rides are separate tickets unless bundled.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Closed from 4 January 2027 until Easter in Tivoli (19–29 March 2027). Christmas Eve and Christmas Day: Verify — one guide reports closure on 24 and 25 December.

Hours: Daily hours: Verify on Tivoli's opening-hours page. Guides report about 11:00–22:00 on weekdays and 11:00–23:00 at weekends.

Best time: A weekday evening, when the lights are on and crowds are lighter.

Meeting point & getting there

Vesterbrogade 3, 1620 Copenhagen V. The box office is at the main entrance on Vesterbrogade. Copenhagen Central Station (trains, Metro, buses) is next to the park.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Physical rating: easy. Garden paths are flat; some rides have age or height limits (Verify per ride).
  • Wheelchairs and companion cards can be collected at the Tivoli Service Center, tel. +45 33 15 10 01.
  • Details for guests with special needs are on Tivoli's accessibility page.
Dress code & rules
  • Your own food and non-alcoholic drinks are allowed. Enjoy them on the Open Air Stage lawn, the gardens along HC Andersens Boulevard or on the benches.
Know before you go
  • Dress warmly: December evenings are cold and dark early.
  • Entrance tickets are dynamic, so check the calendar for the cheapest date.
  • Weekends are the busiest.
  • Tivoli is closed between Halloween (ends 1 November) and the Christmas opening on 13 November.
Cancellation & changes

Cancellation and date-change terms: Verify on Tivoli's terms page before booking.

FAQs

When does Christmas in Tivoli open in 2026?

On 13 November 2026, and it runs to 3 January 2027.

Is it a traditional Christmas market?

Not really. It is Tivoli's gardens with Christmas lights, stalls and rides.

Are the rides open in winter?

Yes. Tivoli states that all rides are open in every season, apart from short weather pauses.

Does the Copenhagen Card cover Christmas in Tivoli?
