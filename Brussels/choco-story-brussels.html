Overview

Choco-Story is a chocolate museum on Rue de l'Étuve, opposite the Manneken Pis. It covers the history of cocoa, how chocolate is made and ends with a hand-made praline demonstration and a tasting.

Groups can add a tablet-making workshop or a guided museum tour.

Opening hours & closed days

Hours: Daily 10:00–18:00. Last entry 17:00 — Verify.

Best time: Morning, before the old-town crowds.

Meeting point & getting there

Rue de l'Étuve / Stoofstraat 41, 1000 Brussels, opposite Manneken Pis. About 5 minutes on foot from the Grand-Place.
