Overview

Bruges is a medieval town with canals, the Markt and Burg squares and the Basilica of the Holy Blood. Direct SNCB trains from Brussels take about 53–60 minutes, with departures every 30 minutes or so.

Guests can travel independently by train or join a guided coach tour from Brussels. Guided tours are sold by private operators.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: none; trains run daily. Bruges sites keep seasonal hours: Verify.

Hours: Trains about every 30 minutes in the day. Bruges sights are generally open from morning to late afternoon: Verify each site.

Best time: Spring or autumn, with an early start.

Meeting point & getting there

Brussels-Central, Brussels-Midi or Brussels-Nord station for the SNCB train; guided-tour meeting points set by the operator: Verify. Bruges station is 15–20 minutes' walk from the Markt.

FAQs

How long is the train?

About 53–60 minutes on a direct service.

Is a guide included on the train?

No. A train-only ticket has no guide.

Is a coach better for groups?

A coach is easier for group logistics and language support: quote on request.

Is Bruges free to visit?

Walking the town is free. Belfry, boat rides and museums charge entry.
