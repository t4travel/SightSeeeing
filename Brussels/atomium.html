Overview

The Atomium is a 102 m steel model of an iron crystal, built for the 1958 World's Fair. Visitors move through connected spheres by escalator and lift, with exhibitions inside and a panorama sphere at the top.

The adult ticket includes entry to the Design Museum Brussels, about 150 m away.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: none in normal operation. The Atomium closes for annual maintenance for about three weeks in winter (19 January to 6 February in 2026); 2027 dates: Verify.

Hours: Daily 10:00–18:00, last entry 17:30. 24 and 31 December: 10:00–16:00. 25 December and 1 January: noon–18:00. Design Museum: daily 11:00–19:00.

Best time: Opening hour, 10:00. Peak crowds run 11:00–15:00.

Meeting point & getting there

Place de l'Atomium 1, 1020 Brussels (Laeken). Take Metro line 6 to Heysel/Heizel, a few minutes' walk; allow 20–30 minutes from the Grand-Place area.

FAQs

Is the Design Museum included?

Yes. The adult ticket includes entry to the Design Museum Brussels.

Is the ticket skip-the-line?

Only partly. An online ticket skips the ticket-office queue, not the queue to enter the Atomium.

Which months are off-limits?

The Atomium closes for maintenance for a few weeks in winter. Check the year's dates.

Is it open on Mondays?

Yes. It is open every day of the week.
