Overview

Mini-Europe is an outdoor park beside the Atomium with more than 350 scale models of European landmarks, including a 4 m Big Ben and a 13 m Eiffel Tower. The Spirit of Europe exhibition covers the history of the EU.

The 2026 season runs from 14 March 2026 to 3 January 2027.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: closed between seasons. The park shuts after 3 January 2027 and reopens in March: Verify the 2027 dates.

Hours: 14 Mar–30 Jun and 1–30 Sep: 9:30–18:00. 1 Jul–31 Aug: 9:30–19:00. 1 Oct 2026–3 Jan 2027: 10:00–18:00. 24 and 31 Dec: 10:00–17:00. The park closes one hour after the ticket office.

Best time: Morning, for cooler weather and smaller crowds.

Meeting point & getting there

Bruparck, Avenue du Football 1, 1020 Brussels (Laeken). Metro line 6 to Heysel/Heizel.
