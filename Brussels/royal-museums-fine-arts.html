Overview

The Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium hold more than 20,000 works from the 15th to the 21st century. The Old Masters Museum covers Bruegel, Rubens and Van Dyck, and the Magritte Museum holds the largest Magritte collection in the world.

A combined ticket covers the permanent collections of the museums that are open. Which museums are open on your date: Verify.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Mondays, and 1 Jan, 1 May, 18 Jun, 1 Nov, 11 Nov and 25 Dec.

Hours: Tue–Fri 10:00–17:00; Sat–Sun 11:00–18:00. Ticket sales end 45 minutes before closing. 24 and 31 Dec close at 15:00.

Best time: Weekday mornings.

Meeting point & getting there

Rue de la Régence 3, 1000 Brussels (Mont des Arts). Short walk from Brussels-Central station and the Royal Quarter.

FAQs

Is the Magritte Museum open on Mondays?

No. The museums are closed on Mondays.

Which ticket covers Magritte?

The combined ticket covers the Old Masters and Magritte museums. Single-museum tickets also exist.

Is it free at any time?

Entry is free on the first Wednesday afternoon of the month, from 13:00 — Verify.

How long should we plan?

Two to three hours covers the highlights of both museums.
