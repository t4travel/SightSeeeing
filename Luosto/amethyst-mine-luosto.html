Overview

The Lampivaara Amethyst Mine is on a hilltop in Luosto where guests hear the story of amethyst and dig their own stone. In summer guided tours start from Lampivaara café every hour (11:00–17:00); in winter you can ski, snowshoe or ride the Amethyst pendolino, with departures from the Luosto centre and Ukko-Luosto parking lot.

Operators also run snowmobile safaris to the mine, guided hikes (Visit Finland lists a 3-hour Amethyst Adventure from €110) and transfers from Rovaniemi.

FAQs

Do I keep the amethyst?

Yes, you take home your own stone.

How long is the visit?

About one hour at the mine.

Can I get there from Rovaniemi?

Yes, by transfer tours (about 6 hours).

Is it open in winter?

Yes, with departures from Luosto centre and Ukko-Luosto.
