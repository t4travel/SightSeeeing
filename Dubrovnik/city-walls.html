Overview

The walls form a circuit of about 1,940 m around the Old Town, built and reinforced between the 12th and 17th centuries. The route climbs to Minčeta Tower, the highest point, with views over the rooftops, Lokrum and the Adriatic.

One ticket allows a single walk of the walls with no re-entry. The same ticket includes Fort Lovrijenac and the Western Outer Walls, which can be visited on another day within 72 hours.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: 25 December only.

Hours: Summer: 8:00–19:30. Winter hours are shorter (about 9:00–15:00 in the file): Verify the date. Last admission is the published closing time.

Best time: Opening time, or two hours before closing. Avoid midday on cruise-ship days.

Meeting point & getting there

Entrances: Pile Gate (west), Ploče Gate (east) and St John's Fort (harbour), Old Town, 20000 Dubrovnik. Ploče and St John's Fort are often quieter than Pile Gate.

FAQs

Does the ticket include Fort Lovrijenac?

Yes. The fort can be used within 72 hours of the ticket's first scan.

Can we leave and re-enter the walls?

No. One ticket allows a single uninterrupted circuit.

Is the Pass a better buy?

The 1-day Dubrovnik Pass is the same €40 and adds museums and city buses.

When is it closed?

Only on 25 December.
