Overview

Lapland Hotels SnowVillage in Lainio, Kittilä, is rebuilt every winter from snow and ice with a new theme. Visitors can walk through the ice exhibition, see snow suites, have dinner at the Ice Restaurant, or stay the night in a snow suite.

It is about 40 minutes by car from Levi and 15 minutes from Ylläs. A bus connection from the Levi and Ylläs resorts runs daily during opening hours (three times a day in 2025–26), and Lapland Safaris runs day, evening and snowmobile excursions from both resorts.
