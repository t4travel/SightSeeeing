Overview

Whitehaven Beach is a 7 km stretch of silica sand on Whitsunday Island. Cruise Whitsundays runs half-day cruises to the southern end and a full-day Chill & Grill cruise that adds the guided walk to Hill Inlet Lookout. SeaLink also runs a Big Island day tour.

The prices below were read from operator pages with different dates and some are older than the current year, so confirm every price before quoting.

  • A vessel change may be needed for some departures.

FAQs

Does the half-day cruise visit Hill Inlet?

No. It visits the southern end of Whitehaven Beach only.

Which tour includes Hill Inlet?

The Chill & Grill cruise and the Big Island day tour.

Is the reef charge included?

Listed separately on some products; Verify.

What if the weather is bad?

The vessel master may change the beach or cancel for safety.
