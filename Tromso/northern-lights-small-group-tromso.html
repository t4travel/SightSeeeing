Overview

Borealis Tour runs small-group aurora chases for up to eight guests in a Mercedes minivan, with thermal suits, a campfire, hot drinks and photographs included.

If the aurora does not appear, guests receive a 50% discount on the next tour. Other small-group options include Polar Adventures minibus tours from NOK 1,759 and NorthernShots’ aurora warranty tour at NOK 2,400.
