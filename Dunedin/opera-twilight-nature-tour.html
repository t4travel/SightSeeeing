Overview

The Twilight Nature Tour runs on the Otago Peninsula in the evening and gives what the operator describes as the best possibility to see yellow-eyed penguins, the hoiho, in their natural habitat. Like the daytime tours, it supports the work of OPERA.

Booking pages for 1 to 25 November 2026 show a single adult price and a single child price on every date.

FAQs

What time does it run?

In the evening, around dusk: the time changes through the year.

How much does it cost?

Adult NZ$65 and child NZ$25 on booking dates in November 2026.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes, from age 5 (ages 5–14 pay a child fare).

What can I see?

Yellow-eyed penguins and other wildlife.
