Overview

Speight’s has brewed beer on the same site on Rattray Street in Dunedin since 1876. The guided tour of about 50 minutes takes visitors through the historic and modern floors of the brewery, with the story of the brand, the ingredients and the brewing process, and finishes with up to 25 to 30 minutes of tasting in the tasting room.

Allow about 1 hour 15 minutes in total. The tour route has about 200 stairs and a moderate level of fitness is recommended.
