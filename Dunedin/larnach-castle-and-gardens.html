Overview

Larnach Castle is a 20-minute drive from central Dunedin on the Otago Peninsula. A self-guided visit covers the castle including the tower, the ballroom café, the gift shop, the gardens, the historic stables and outbuildings, with a brochure in several languages. Guided tours are offered daily at extra cost: ask at the gatehouse.

The operator’s published prices were valid from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026 and new rates from 1 October 2026 were not found, so confirm the current prices.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open 365 days

Hours: Open every day of the year; opening hours differ in summer and winter, so check the hours for your date.

Best time: Morning, to explore the gardens before lunch.

Meeting point & getting there

Larnach Castle & Gardens, 145 Camp Road, Otago Peninsula, about a 20-minute drive from the Dunedin city centre. There is no public transport to the castle.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Moderate: the castle is accessed by stairs only; the gardens have gravel, uneven ground, cobblestones, paths, lawn and steps.
  • Dogs are allowed in the grounds and gardens on a leash, but not in the castle, accommodation or outbuildings.
Know before you go
  • Allow at least two hours.
  • Tickets can be used any time during opening hours.
  • Ask about guided tours at the gatehouse.
Cancellation & changes

Admission tickets are for entry during opening hours. Confirm the cancellation terms when you book through a reseller.

FAQs

How much is entry?

Castle & Gardens NZ$48 for adults and NZ$16.50 for children 5–14, valid to 30 September 2026; confirm the current price.

Is there public transport?

No, the castle is a self-drive or tour destination.

Can I bring a dog?

Yes in the grounds and gardens on a leash; not inside the castle.

Is the castle accessible?

The castle is accessed by stairs only.
