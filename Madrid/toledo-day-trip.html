Overview

Toledo's old town holds a cathedral, two medieval synagogues, a mosque and El Greco's work. The Renfe Avant from Madrid Atocha takes about 33 minutes.

Prices below are guide-site figures and read Verify; confirm on Renfe and the monuments' own sites.
