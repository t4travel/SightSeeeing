Overview

The Thyssen-Bornemisza collection was assembled by one family and runs from 13th-century paintings to Pop Art. It fills the gap between the Prado and the Reina Sofía.

The ticket includes the permanent collection, the Carmen Thyssen Collection and current temporary exhibitions.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: 1 January, 1 May and 25 December. Open 10:00–15:00 on 24 and 31 December.

Hours: 1 Sep–30 Jun: Mon 12:00–16:00 (free); Tue–Fri and Sun 10:00–19:00; Sat 10:00–23:00. 1 Jul–31 Aug: Tue–Fri 10:00–21:00. Last admission 30 minutes before closing.

Best time: Monday–Friday 14:00–16:00 is quietest, per the museum.

FAQs

When is it free?

Mondays 12:00–16:00 and Saturdays 21:00–23:00. No ticket is needed.

Is it open on Mondays?

Yes, from 12:00 to 16:00, free of charge.

How long does a visit take?

About 2.5 hours, plus 50 minutes for the ground-floor temporary exhibition.

Is there a group price?

Yes, €12 per person for groups of 7 or more.
