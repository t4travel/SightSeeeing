Overview

Retiro Park was a royal garden until 1868 and is part of the Paseo del Prado and Buen Retiro landscape listed by UNESCO. Locals use it for walking, rowing and Sunday strolls.

The Palacio de Cristal and Palacio de Velázquez are listed as temporarily closed on the Reina Sofía site, so confirm access before promising a visit inside.

FAQs

Is Retiro free?

Yes, entry to the park is free.

Can we go inside the Crystal Palace?

It is listed as temporarily closed. Verify before travel.

How long do we need?

About 1.5–3 hours for the lake, monument and gardens.

Is it near the museums?

Yes, the park sits next to the Prado and Paseo del Prado.
