Overview

The old centre links Puerta del Sol, the Plaza Mayor and the Plaza de Oriente in an easy walking loop. Puerta del Sol holds the Kilometre Zero marker for Spain's roads.

This is a free self-guided route. Guided versions exist but prices were not confirmed.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily. Palace guard-change schedule: Verify.

Hours: Outdoor route, open at all times. Shops and cafés vary.

Best time: Morning for fewer crowds; evenings for atmosphere.

Meeting point & getting there

Start at Puerta del Sol, Madrid (metro Sol, lines 1, 2, 3). Finish near Ópera metro.
