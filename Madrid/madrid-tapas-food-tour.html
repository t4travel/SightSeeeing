Overview

A guided walk through Madrid's old centre with stops at family-run tapas bars, including the garlic-shrimp bar. Public tours serve about 9 tapas and 4 drinks.

Private groups of 13 to 60 can request a dedicated tour at €89 per person for 2.5 hours.
