Overview

The Royal Palace is the official residence of the Spanish crown, used today for state ceremonies. Visitors walk through the throne room, the Hall of Mirrors and the banquet hall.

A self-guided visit takes about 45 minutes. Guided tours by Patrimonio Nacional staff and a combined ticket with the Gallery of the Royal Collections are available.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: 1 and 6 January, 1 May, 25 December, plus 24 and 31 December from 15:00. Extra closures for official events can happen without notice.

Hours: October–March: Monday–Saturday 10:00–18:00, Sunday 10:00–16:00. April–September: Monday–Saturday 10:00–19:00, Sunday 10:00–16:00. The box office closes 1 hour early. English guided tours: Mon–Sat 10:45, 11:45, 13:45, 15:15; Sun 10:45, 11:45, 13:45, 14:15.

Best time: Opening time, or a guided slot at 10:45.

Meeting point & getting there

Calle de Bailén s/n, 28071 Madrid. Since 20 August access is through the Almudena Cathedral esplanade and Plaza de la Armería. Metro: Ópera (lines 2 and 5). Train: Príncipe Pío. Buses 3, 25, 39, 148.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Physical rating: moderate. Visit lasts about 45 minutes self-guided.
  • Accessible for reduced mobility; wheelchairs are available at the palace.
Dress code & rules
  • Large backpacks, suitcases and bags are not recommended because of works.
  • No tripods or selfie sticks. Photos without flash for personal use only.
Know before you go
  • For guided visits, arrive at the preferred door 15 minutes before and be at the hall meeting point 5 minutes before.
  • The Royal Armoury is closed to the public until further notice.
  • Closed at short notice for state functions.

FAQs

How long does it take?

About 45 minutes self-guided; allow 2–2.5 hours with a guide or the Gallery.

Are there guided tours in English?

Yes, four times a day Monday–Saturday and three times on Sundays.

Is there a free-entry time?

Yes, Monday–Thursday late afternoon, but only for EU and Latin American passport or residency holders.

Can groups book through agencies?

Yes. The official site lists a travel-agency group rate.
