Overview

The Royal Site of San Lorenzo de El Escorial is a 16th-century palace-monastery built for Philip II, with a basilica, royal library and the Pantheon of Kings.

From 2 October to 31 December 2026 the Painting and Architecture Museums are free and also included in the monastery ticket.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Mondays; 1 and 6 January, 1 May, 24, 25 and 31 December.

Hours: October–March: Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–18:00 (last access 16:45). April–September: 10:00–19:00. English guided tour Tue–Sun 10:45.

Best time: Morning guided slot at 10:45.

Meeting point & getting there

Calle de Juan de Borbón y Battenberg s/n, 28200 San Lorenzo de El Escorial. Tickets: Visitor Reception Centre, Avenida de Juan de Borbón y Battenberg 4. Commuter train C-8; buses 661 and 664 from Moncloa.
