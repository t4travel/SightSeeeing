Overview

Several operators run cruises on the Bosphorus. The Şehir Hatları public ferry is cheapest: a six-hour long tour from Eminönü to Anadolu Kavağı near the Black Sea, and a two-hour short tour in summer only. Turyol and Dentur run 90-minute tourist boats, and private yachts run sunset cruises.

A guide helps most on private boats. Public ferries have no commentary.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily. Weather can cancel private sailings, and the short ferry tour does not run in winter.

Hours: Şehir Hatları long cruise: departs Eminönü at 10:35 year-round (arrive 30–45 minutes early in summer). Other operators run most of the day.

Best time: Sunset in May to October, or the long cruise on a clear weekday.

Meeting point & getting there

Eminönü Bosphorus Pier (Boğaz İskelesi), right of the Galata Bridge, or Kabataş for some operators. Nearest stop: Eminönü (T1 tram).

Know before you go
  • Some pier ticket windows take cash only: check payment methods.
  • The long ferry stops about 2 hours at Anadolu Kavağı.
  • Public ferries are crowded in peak season.

FAQs

Which is the cheapest cruise?

The Şehir Hatları public ferry.

Is there a sunset option?

Yes, on private yachts and the summer Moonlight Cruise.

How long is the long ferry?

About 6 hours with a 2-hour stop.

Is there commentary?

On most tourist boats, not on the public ferry.
