Overview

Hagia Sophia was built as a church in the 6th century, became a mosque after 1453, a museum in 1934, and a mosque again in 2020. Foreign visitors now enter by a dedicated upper-gallery route, where the Byzantine mosaics are still visible, and look down over the prayer hall.

The Blue Mosque faces it across Sultanahmet Square. It is free, fully open after its long restoration (public reopening in April 2023), and closes to tourists around each of the five daily prayers.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: The Blue Mosque closes to tourists around each of the five daily prayers (each pause is often 60–90 minutes) and on Fridays until about 14:30. Hagia Sophia’s own closed days and holiday closures: Verify.

Hours: Blue Mosque: daily from about 08:30 until roughly an hour before dusk, with prayer pauses. Hagia Sophia hours: Verify at the official ticket channel.

Best time: Early morning, around 09:00, and away from prayer times.

Meeting point & getting there

Sultanahmet Square, Fatih, Istanbul. Take the T1 tram to Sultanahmet; the square is a short walk from the stop.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Moderate. A long walk on stone floors and a worn ramp to the upper gallery.
  • Step-free access to the gallery: Verify.

Dress code & rules
  • Cover shoulders and knees in both buildings. Women cover their hair.
  • Shoes are removed at the Blue Mosque and in the prayer areas of Hagia Sophia.
Know before you go
  • Prayer times change daily, so build a buffer into the itinerary.
  • Without a guide or a fast-entry ticket the Hagia Sophia queue can run 1–2 hours.
  • Istanbul museums stopped accepting cash at entrances on 1 August 2025, so guests need a payment card.
  • Indian-passport guests need a Turkish visa before travel. The e-Visa (evisa.gov.tr) is open only to those holding a valid Schengen, US, UK or Ireland visa or residence permit, and is single-entry for 30 days. Otherwise a sticker visa is needed: Verify current rules and fees.

FAQs

Is the Blue Mosque free?

Yes. It needs no ticket, but it closes around the five daily prayers and on Friday mornings.

What does the Hagia Sophia ticket cover?

The upper-gallery visitor route, at €25 for foreign visitors.

Is the Blue Mosque still under renovation?

It reopened after its long restoration in April 2023. Check the day’s notice at the courtyard.

Is there a dress code?

Yes: covered shoulders and knees for everyone, and a head covering for women.
