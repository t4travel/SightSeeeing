Overview

A Byzantine water reservoir beneath Sultanahmet, reached by 52 steps. Rows of marble columns stand in shallow water, and two Medusa heads support columns at the far end.

Day entry runs about 09:00–18:30, and a separate Night Shift programme runs 19:30–22:00. The cistern is run by the city, not the Ministry of Culture, so the Museum Pass is not valid.

FAQs

Is the Museum Pass accepted?

No. The cistern is run by the city.

Is the night ticket worth it?

It costs about double and is far quieter, with better lighting for photos.

How long is the visit?

30 to 60 minutes.

Can children enter?
