Overview

The seat of the Ottoman court for about 400 years. The visit covers four courtyards, the Harem, the Imperial Treasury and the sacred relics. Hagia Irene, the Byzantine church in the first courtyard, is part of the same complex.

Sources disagree on how the ticket is sold. One 2026 guide gives a single combined ticket for palace, Harem and Hagia Irene at TRY 2,750 (about €55). Another gives separate palace and Harem tickets at about TRY 1,500 and TRY 1,000. Confirm the structure before quoting.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Closed every Tuesday. Mornings of Ramazan Bayramı and Kurban Bayramı are closed: Verify dates.

Hours: In summer (mid-April to end-October) about 09:00–18:45, with last entry about 18:00. In winter it closes earlier: Verify.

Best time: Opening time, and not on Mondays, which are crowded after the Tuesday closure.

Meeting point & getting there

Topkapı Palace, Sultanahmet, Fatih, Istanbul. The Imperial Gate is behind Hagia Sophia; take the T1 tram to Gülhane or Sultanahmet.

Dress code & rules
  • Dress modestly: covered shoulders and knees. Bags are scanned at the entrance.
Know before you go
  • Plan 2.5 to 4 hours and the Harem separately if sold separately.
  • Prayer-time buffers do not apply, but the Treasury can be crowded mid-morning.
  • Istanbul museums stopped accepting cash at entrances on 1 August 2025, so guests need a payment card.
  • Museum Pass Istanbul is reported to cover the palace: Verify scope.

2.5 to 4 hours with the Harem and Treasury.
