Overview

Büyükada, Heybeliada and two smaller islands lie off the Asian shore. Public Şehir Hatları ferries take 60 to 90 minutes to Büyükada, stopping at the other islands on the way. The islands are car-free: visitors walk, cycle or take a horse carriage.

Fares and tour prices were not confirmed. The islands are busiest on summer weekends, so a weekday is calmer.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily. Ferries run to a timetable that differs by season.

Hours: First ferry out in the morning, late-afternoon ferry back: Verify and note the last realistic return.

Best time: Weekday in spring or autumn.

Meeting point & getting there

Kabataş or Eminönü pier for ferries: Verify the departure pier for your boat.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Moderate. Hills on Büyükada, and no cars.
  • Carriages and bikes are available.
Know before you go
  • Missing the last ferry back is the main risk.
  • Carriages and bikes are paid separately.
  • Busy on summer weekends.

FAQs

How long is the ferry?

About 60 to 90 minutes to Büyükada.

Are there cars?

No. The islands are car-free.

Which day is best?

A weekday.

Is it good for groups?

Yes, with a guide and a fixed return ferry.
