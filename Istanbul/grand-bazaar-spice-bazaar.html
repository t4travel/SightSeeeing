Overview

The Grand Bazaar has about 61 covered streets and 4,000 shops. The Spice Bazaar is smaller, built in 1664, and sells spices, Turkish delight and teas. Neither charges an entry fee: anyone selling “Grand Bazaar entry” is selling a guided tour.

A guide helps with orientation and with haggling. Beware of strangers who offer to take you to a shop.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: The Grand Bazaar is closed on Sundays and on the first days of Ramazan and Kurban Bayramı. The Spice Bazaar opens on Sundays.

Hours: Grand Bazaar: Monday–Saturday, about 08:30–19:00 (09:00–19:00 per another guide). Spice Bazaar: Verify.

Best time: Mornings between 09:00 and 11:00, before the crowds. Saturday afternoon is busiest.

Meeting point & getting there

Grand Bazaar, Beyazıt gate or Nuruosmaniye gate, Fatih. Beyazıt–Kapalıçarşı (T1 tram). The Spice Bazaar is by the Galata Bridge at Eminönü, about 15 minutes’ walk.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy but crowded, with uneven floors and slopes.
  • Wheelchair access is limited in the older lanes.
Know before you go
  • Closed on Sundays: the Spice Bazaar is the Sunday substitute.
  • Haggle politely, and do not accept shop tours from strangers.
  • Scarves for mosque visits are sold inside.

FAQs

Is the Grand Bazaar open on Sunday?

No. It is closed on Sundays.

Do we need a ticket?

No. Entry to both markets is free.

Should we take a guide?

It helps with orientation and with avoiding tourist-trap shops.

What is the Spice Bazaar famous for?

Spices, Turkish delight and teas.
