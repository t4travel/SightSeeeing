Overview

Icehotel in Jukkasjärvi is rebuilt every winter from ice cut from the Torne river and has art suites designed by invited artists, a main hall and an ice bar. A year-round part, Icehotel 365, keeps art suites, a bar and a sauna open in all seasons.

The 37th edition, Icehotel 37, opens on Friday 11 December 2026 (opening ceremony at 18:00). Day-visit tickets shown on the official site are valid until December 2026; the winter 2026–27 price was not published when I checked.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Icehotel 365 open all year; winter Icehotel opens 11 Dec 2026

Hours: Icehotel 365: open daily year-round. Icehotel 37 opens 11 December 2026. The Old Homestead restaurant opens 11 December 2026 and closes 28 March 2027. Reception lobby open 24 hours. Verify winter day-visit hours.

Best time: Daytime to see the art; evening for the atmosphere.
