Overview

Icehotel offers cold accommodation (ice art suites and cold rooms made of ice, with thermal sleeping bags) and warm accommodation (Arctic cabins and Icehotel 365 rooms). Winter packages add activities such as dog sledding, snowmobile safaris and northern lights tours.

The opening offer for Icehotel 37 is a two-night package from 10 to 12 December 2026 with airport pickup by dog sled, a northern lights safari, ice sculpting, dog sledding and the guided opening tour. Ice-room rates for 2026–27 were not published; one blog indicated from about SEK 6,000 for a night (unverified).

FAQs

Is it very cold?

Cold rooms stay below freezing and guests use thermal sleeping bags.

Can I choose a warm room?

Yes, Arctic cabins and Icehotel 365 rooms.

What does the opening offer include?

Two nights with activities on 10–12 December 2026.

Are rates published?

Not for 2026–27 when checked.
