About Geneva

Geneva is the Swiss capital of diplomacy and watchmaking, on the western end of Lake Geneva with views of Mont Blanc. The Jet d'Eau fountain, the Old Town around St Pierre Cathedral, the Reformation Wall, the United Nations at the Palais des Nations, CERN's Science Gateway and the Patek Philippe Museum are its headline sights, with the Salève cable car for views over the lake.

Getting around and passes

  • Swiss Travel Pass (3, 4, 6, 8 or 15 consecutive days; Flex within one month): unlimited rail, bus and boat, free entry to many museums, 50% off most mountain railways.
  • Swiss Half Fare Card: CHF 150 for one month, half price on most travel.
  • Geneva City Pass: 24, 48 or 72 hours with museums, the Mont Salève cable car, lake cruise and guided tours, with an optional public-transport version (price: Verify).
  • Hotel guests normally receive a free local transport ticket (Verify).
