Overview

A half-day coach trip down the Athenian Riviera to the Temple of Poseidon, built around 440 BC on a headland about 70 km from Athens. Departure times move through the year to match sunset.

Some products include the site ticket and some do not, so check the option before selling.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily. The site closes at sunset, with last entry about 20 minutes before.

Hours: Departures follow sunset: 16:00 (14 Sep–25 Oct), 15:00 (26–31 Oct), 14:30 (1 Nov–18 Jan 2027). Reconfirm the exact time before travel.

Best time: Arrive well before sunset to see the temple in light and avoid the last-minute crush.

Meeting point & getting there

Meeting point depends on the product; many start at a central Athens pickup point. The site is at Cape Sounion, Sounio 195 00, about 70 km from Athens.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy. Flat paths on the promontory, with some uneven ground and wind.
  • Coach seating; long sitting time each way.
Dress code & rules
  • Do not climb on or touch the columns; the temple is fenced.
Know before you go
  • The site fee is often paid on arrival, not bundled — check the option.
  • Wind can be strong at the cape; bring a layer.
  • Cloud can hide the sunset, so set expectations.
Cancellation & changes

Resellers show free cancellation from 24 to 72 hours before departure, depending on the product — Verify before selling.
