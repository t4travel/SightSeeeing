Overview

The great Renaissance collection of Florence, including Botticelli’s Birth of Venus and works by Leonardo, Raphael and Michelangelo. Allow 2 to 3 hours for the highlights.

Since 13 October 2025 every ticket carries the visitor’s name, which must match the passport or ID shown at the door. A booking surcharge applies to tickets bought before the day of the visit. Slots sell out one to two weeks ahead in April–September.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Closed every Monday, 1 January and 25 December. Free entry on the first Sunday of the month: no advance booking is possible on those days.

Hours: Tuesday to Sunday, from 08:15. The ticket office closes at 17:30. Verify closing time and last entry on uffizi.it.

Best time: The 08:15 opening slot, or the afternoon ticket from 16:00.

Meeting point & getting there

Piazzale degli Uffizi 6, 50122 Florence. Door 3 for voucher exchange, Door 1 for security. It is a short walk from Piazza della Signoria and the Duomo.

Dress code & rules
  • Names on the tickets must match each visitor’s passport or ID. Names cannot be changed freely: Verify.
Know before you go
  • Book as early as dates are firm, as slots sell out in peak season.
  • Skip-the-line applies to any pre-booked timed ticket. There is no separate fast lane.
  • A free Sunday means long queues and no booking.
Cancellation & changes

Official tickets are named and timed. Change and refund terms: Verify with the ticket seller.

FAQs

Is Monday really closed?

Yes, every Monday. Plan the Uffizi and the Accademia on other days.

Which ticket is cheapest?

The afternoon ticket from 16:00, €16 on the day.

Can the Vasari Corridor be added?

Only on special opening days, entered two hours after the Uffizi slot: Verify dates.

Must every guest carry ID?

Yes. Tickets are in the visitor’s name and checked at the door.
