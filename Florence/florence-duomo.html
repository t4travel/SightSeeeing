Overview

The cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore is free to enter. A ticket is needed for the rest of the Duomo complex: the dome climb, Giotto’s Bell Tower, the Baptistery, the Museo dell’Opera del Duomo and the Santa Reparata crypt.

Only the Brunelleschi Pass includes the dome. The climb is 463 steps, has no lift, and is booked for a fixed time slot when the pass is bought. The pass is valid for three days.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: The dome is closed for maintenance 16–20 November 2026. The cathedral has restricted access on Sundays and for services.

Hours: Hours differ by site and day: Verify at duomo.firenze.it. The Museo dell’Opera del Duomo is reported open 08:30–19:00, closed the first Tuesday of the month.

Best time: First dome slot of the day, and weekday mornings.

Meeting point & getting there

Piazza del Duomo, 50122 Florence. Ticket office: Piazza San Giovanni. It is a 10-minute walk from Santa Maria Novella station.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Strenuous. 463 narrow steps with no lift.
  • Not suited to claustrophobia or limited mobility. The cathedral floor is step-free: Verify.
Dress code & rules
  • Cover shoulders and knees to enter the cathedral. Sleeveless tops and short shorts are refused.
Know before you go
  • The dome slot is fixed when you buy and cannot be moved.
  • Small bags only on the dome stairs.
  • Touts in the piazza sell the same tickets at a markup.
Cancellation & changes

Dome slots are fixed at purchase. Reseller tickets may offer free cancellation: Verify.

FAQs

Is the cathedral free?

Yes. Only the dome, bell tower, Baptistery, museum and crypt need a pass.

Which pass includes the dome?

Only the Brunelleschi Pass, €30 for adults.

Is the Duomo on the Firenze Card?

No. It needs a separate pass.

Is the dome open every day?

Not on 16–20 November 2026, and times change for religious events.
