Overview

The Ponte Vecchio, lined with jewellers’ shops, is the oldest bridge across the Arno. The Oltrarno on the south bank is Florence’s artisan quarter, with workshops, Santo Spirito and the Pitti Palace.

The Vasari Corridor above the bridge opens on special days only, on a separate timed ticket tied to an Uffizi slot.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open 24 hours. Workshops and shops close on Sundays and for lunch.

Hours: Bridge: always open. Shops: usually 10:00–19:00: Verify.

Best time: Sunrise for bridge photos, and evening for the Oltrarno.

Meeting point & getting there

Ponte Vecchio, Florence. Start from Piazza della Signoria, a 5-minute walk. Cross the bridge to the Oltrarno.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy, flat on the bridge. Cobbled streets and some small steps in the Oltrarno.
  • Wheelchair access is fair on the main streets: Verify.
Know before you go
  • Peak hours on the bridge are crowded, and shops sell at jewellers’ prices.
  • Pair the walk with the Pitti Palace or Piazzale Michelangelo.
  • The Vasari Corridor is not open on most days.
