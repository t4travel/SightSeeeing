Overview

Phillip Island is about 90 minutes’ drive from Melbourne. At dusk the little penguins come ashore at Summerlands Beach. Phillip Island Nature Parks runs the viewing.

Public transport is limited, so many visitors join a day tour. The official pages list slightly different fares: Verify.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily; parade is nightly

Hours: Visitor centre opens at 12:00 daily. Plan to arrive at least one hour before the expected penguin arrival time. Verify.

Best time: Late afternoon until dusk.

Meeting point & getting there

Penguin Parade, Summerlands, Phillip Island VIC 3922, about 90 minutes’ drive from Melbourne. Coach tours collect from the CBD; meeting point is in the booking.

Know before you go
  • Arrive early for the best seats.
  • Evenings are cold and windy; bring layers.
  • Many tours return late at night.

FAQs

How far is it from Melbourne?

About 90 minutes by car.

When do the penguins arrive?

At dusk, so the time changes with the season.

Can I photograph the penguins?

No, photography is not permitted.

How do I get there without a car?

By coach tour from the CBD.
