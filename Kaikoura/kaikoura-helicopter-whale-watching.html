Overview

Helicopter operators in Kaikōura combine a search for whales above the Kaikōura Whale Sanctuary with views of the Seaward Kaikōura mountains rising to over 2,500 metres, and on some flights a landing at an alpine site.

Prices below are from a tour comparison site: a 1-hour helicopter with an alpine landing at NZ$750 and a 45-minute whale-watching helicopter at NZ$850. South Pacific Whale Watch lists its flights from NZ$495 to NZ$1,575 for adults. Confirm the operator’s current fare.

Meeting point & getting there

The operator has an office in the Kaikōura township; flights depart from Kaikōura Airport, about 8 minutes’ drive from town. A full refund is given if the operator cancels for reasons outside its control.
