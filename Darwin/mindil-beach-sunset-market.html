Overview

The Mindil Beach Sunset Market runs on Thursday and Sunday evenings through the dry season, from late April to late October. The 2026 season closes on Thursday 29 October.

More than 150 food and specialty stalls line the beach, with live entertainment and sunset viewing. Entry is free. Reserved Sunset Lounge seating is sold separately from A$20.
