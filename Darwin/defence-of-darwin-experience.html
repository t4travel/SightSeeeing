Overview

The Darwin Military Museum is at East Point Reserve, about 10 minutes’ drive from the city centre. It is in the original concrete command post and includes the Defence of Darwin Experience and the Darwin Holocaust Memorial and Education Centre.

An older source listed A$14 adult and A$5.50 child, but that figure may be out of date, so the current price should be confirmed: Verify. Entry tickets can be used on any day within 30 days of purchase.

Meeting point & getting there

Darwin Military Museum, 253 Alec Fong Lim Drive, East Point NT 0820. About a 10-minute drive from the city; public transport is slow, so a taxi is easier.
