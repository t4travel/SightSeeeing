Overview

Wētā Workshop Experiences is a 90-minute guided tour of the film effects studio at 1 Weka Street in Miramar, with free entry to the Wētā Cave shop. A half-day tour combines a Lord of the Rings locations tour with Weta Workshop.

The Weta Workshop tour price in New Zealand dollars was not available. The reseller prices below are for the half-day tour that includes Weta: Verify.
