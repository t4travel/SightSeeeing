Overview

Matiu / Somes Island is a predator-free island in Wellington Harbour managed by the Department of Conservation. A ferry from the city crosses the harbour, and visitors walk the island tracks to see native wildlife and historic sites.

Guides list an adult return fare of NZ$38 including a NZ$6 DOC fee, and a child fare of NZ$16 plus the NZ$6 DOC fee: Verify.
