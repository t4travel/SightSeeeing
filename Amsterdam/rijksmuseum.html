Overview

The Rijksmuseum holds Rembrandt's Night Watch, Vermeer's Milkmaid and Hals portraits alongside Delft Blue and Golden Age galleries. All tickets are timed and sold online only; the museum does not sell tickets in person.

FAQs

Can I buy tickets at the door?

No. The museum sells timed tickets online only; same-day tickets are limited to remaining capacity.

How long should I allow?

2–3 hours for highlights; 4–5 hours with the Asian Pavilion and special exhibitions.

Are children charged?

Visitors under 18 are free; a free ticket still needs a time slot.

Is the guided tour worth it?

It focuses on the Night Watch, the Vermeers and the Golden Age rooms with an art historian, in about 2 hours.
