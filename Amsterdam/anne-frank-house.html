Overview

The Anne Frank House preserves the hiding place of Anne Frank, her family and four others during WWII. Tickets are sold only on annefrank.org — there is no door sale and no third-party entry product. Third-party offers claiming entry are not official.

FAQs

Where do I buy tickets?

Only on annefrank.org; the site lists two ticket options — check them at booking.

When do tickets go on sale?

Every Tuesday 10:00 Amsterdam time for the week six weeks later; extra tickets are sometimes released for the following week.

Can I buy on the day?

Not at the door. Check the official site for any released same-week tickets.

Is it suitable for children?

Not recommended for young children; under-10s are charged a nominal fee (Verify).
