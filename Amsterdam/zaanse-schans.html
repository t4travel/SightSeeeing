Overview

Zaanse Schans is an open-air village of working windmills on the Zaan river. Walking the village is free; you pay to enter windmills and museums, usually via the Zaanse Schans Card. A pre-booked entry fee of €17.50 was announced for spring 2026 — Verify whether it is in force.

Know before you go
  • The I amsterdam City Card covers the windmills and museums
  • Museumkaart covers museums but not windmills
  • Card vouchers are exchanged for a physical card at the Zaans Museum desk
Cancellation & changes

FAQs

Is Zaanse Schans free?

The village is free to walk; windmills and museums are ticketed.

How do I get there?

About 17 minutes by train from Amsterdam Centraal, or an organised tour.

Which ticket is best?

The Zaanse Schans Card covers the museums and two windmills for one price.

Can I combine it with Keukenhof?

Yes — a classic full-day Holland pairing in spring.
