Overview

The Swiss Travel Pass covers unlimited travel on trains, buses and boats across Switzerland, local transport in over 90 towns and cities, and free entry to more than 500 museums. It gives 50% off most mountain railways (for example Schilthorn, Titlis, Glacier 3000, Gornergrat and First) and 25% off the Jungfraujoch top section. Mount Rigi is 100% included.

Every duration comes in 2nd and 1st class, as a consecutive pass, as a Flex pass (3, 4, 6, 8 or 15 travel days within one month) and as a Youth version for travellers up to their 25th birthday at 30% less. The Swiss Half Fare Card costs CHF 150 for one month and halves most fares.

FAQs

Which durations exist?

3, 4, 6, 8 and 15 consecutive days, plus Flex passes for the same numbers of days within one month.

How much is a 4-day 2nd-class pass?

CHF 309 in 2026 (1st class CHF 492).

Is the Jungfraujoch free?

No: 25% off the top section; the Jungfrau Travel Pass sets a connecting fare.

Is there a youth discount?

Yes, 30% for travellers up to their 25th birthday.
