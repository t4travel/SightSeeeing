About Bern

Bern is Switzerland's federal city, with a UNESCO-listed medieval Old Town of arcades and fountains, the Zytglogge clock tower, the Federal Palace (Bundeshaus), the Bear Park on the Aare, the Rose Garden view, Einstein's house and the Paul Klee Centre. It is calm and walkable, and little known in the Indian market, which makes it a fresh addition to Zurich, Lucerne and Interlaken itineraries.

Getting there

  • Zurich Airport about 1 hr by rail; Lucerne 1 hr; Interlaken about 1 hr; Basel about 1 hr (Verify).
  • Bern is a rail hub: GoldenPass, Lötschberg and Gruyères routes all start here.

Seasons

  • Late November to Christmas: Christmas markets (dates: Verify).
  • May to September: Aare floating and swimming, terraces and gardens.
  • Museums close on Mondays (Kunstmuseum Bern is closed Monday).

Tips for Indian travellers

  • Bern is easy and quiet: a good half-day stop between Lucerne and Interlaken.
  • Parliament tours are free but must be booked: they are suspended when Parliament sits.
