Overview

Featherdale is in Doonside, west of Sydney near Blacktown. It is known for hands-on wildlife encounters such as feeding kangaroos and wallabies.

Meeting point & getting there

Featherdale Wildlife Park, 217–229 Kildare Road, Doonside NSW. By train to Blacktown station, then bus 729 for about 10 minutes.
