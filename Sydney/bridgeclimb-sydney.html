Overview

BridgeClimb leads small groups up the upper arch of the Sydney Harbour Bridge, with 1,332 stairs and 360° views from the summit, 134 m above the harbour.

Prices change with the climb type, the day of the week and the time of day. Weekend climbs cost A$10 more for adults.

  • Strenuous: 1,332 stairs and about 3 to 3.5 hours. Good general fitness needed.

Know before you go
  • Prices vary by date, so check your exact date at checkout.
  • Book early for twilight, dawn and weekend climbs.
  • Weather can affect climbs; policy: Verify.
  • The pedestrian footway across the Bridge is free; the Pylon Lookout is a cheaper view.

FAQs

How many steps are there?

1,332 stairs on the upper arch Summit Climb.

Which climb is cheapest?

The Summit Climb at night: A$325 on weekdays and A$335 on weekends.

Can children climb?

Yes, if they are over 1.2 m tall and with an adult, with a maximum of three children per adult.

Is there a cheaper way up?

The Pylon Lookout has views from 87 m for A$29.95, with 200 stairs.
