Overview

The Opera House sits on Bennelong Point in Sydney Harbour. The official one-hour tour takes you inside some of the venues and theatres and explains how the building was designed and built.

Pre-booking is cheaper than buying on the day, and slots can sell out in peak periods.

Your visit, step by step
Arrive and check in
Go to the Welcome Centre on the Lower Concourse and show your booking. Bags may be inspected or cloaked first, so allow extra time.
Guided tour
A guide leads the group through the building and some of its venues.
Finish
The tour lasts about 1 hour. The forecourt and harbour walk are free to explore afterwards.

Meeting point & getting there

Welcome Centre, Lower Concourse, Sydney Opera House, Bennelong Point, Sydney NSW 2000. Catch a bus, train or ferry to Circular Quay, then walk 7–10 minutes.

FAQs

Is it cheaper to pre-book?

Yes. The adult tour is A$50 pre-booked and A$55 on the day.

Can I see inside the Opera House without a tour?

The forecourt is open to walk around. To see the venues you need a tour or a performance ticket.

How long is the tour?

About 1 hour.

Are tours offered in other languages?

French, German and Japanese tours are listed on the official site. Check the timetable for your date.
