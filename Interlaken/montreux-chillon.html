Overview

The GoldenPass Express runs directly between Interlaken Ost and Montreux in about 3 hours 15 minutes, with no change at Zweisimmen, through Lake Thun, the Simmental valley and down to Lake Geneva. Other options change at Zweisimmen onto the GoldenPass Panoramic or the Belle Époque train.

Chillon Castle is on the lake shore between Montreux and Villeneuve. It has wall paintings from the 14th century, underground vaults, the Counts of Savoy's chapel, and the cell of François Bonivard. Eight short films play on terminals through the castle. It is a long day trip, so many guests stay a night in Montreux.

Meeting point & getting there

Montreux railway station; Chillon is at Avenue de Chillon 21, Veytaux, about 4 km from Montreux. Interlaken Ost to Montreux by GoldenPass Express takes about 3 hrs 15 min.

Know before you go
  • The same-day return is tight: the last GoldenPass Express back leaves Montreux at 14:33 per one timetable listing. Check the final connection or stay a night.
  • Seat reservations are optional but recommended; the Express can sell out.
  • Swiss Travel Pass holders must contact the castle at least 48 hours ahead (Verify).
  • Selfie sticks are not allowed in the castle and drones cannot be flown over the grounds.

FAQs

How long does the GoldenPass Express take?

About 3 hours 15 minutes between Interlaken Ost and Montreux.

Can I visit as a day trip from Interlaken?

Yes, but the last return departure from Montreux on the Express is early (14:33 per one listing, Verify). Many guests stay overnight.

How much is Chillon Castle?

CHF 13.50 for adults on the tourism listing, CHF 15 on another source; children CHF 7. It is free with the Swiss Travel Pass.

Do I need a seat reservation?

Not for ordinary 2nd class, but it is recommended: CHF 20 on the Express, CHF 49 compulsory for Prestige.
