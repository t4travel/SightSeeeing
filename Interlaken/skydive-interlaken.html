Overview

Skydive Interlaken runs tandem skydives from a helicopter above the Jungfrau region, landing at its dropzone in Wilderswil. Jumps range from the standard Interlaken jump from about 14,000 ft to the Eiger Jump in Lauterbrunnen and the High Altitude Helicopter HALO Jump.

The HALO jump is a private package for one or two people. A 20-minute scenic flight climbs to the Jungfraujoch and back, oxygen masks are used above 8,000 ft, and the jump from about 18,000 ft gives roughly 80 seconds of freefall against 40–45 seconds on standard jumps.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Physical rating: high. Good general health required; the operator may decline a jump if it has a safety concern.
  • Operator limits on the HALO page: women up to 90 kg and BMI 27.5, men up to 100 kg and BMI 30, height up to 195 cm. Minimum age 12; under 18 needs a parent's consent.
Dress code & rules
  • Weight, height and BMI limits above apply: verify the limits for each jump type with the operator.
  • No alcohol or drugs before the jump; the operator may refuse guests it believes have consumed them.
  • Guests may not carry their own cameras or recording devices: the instructor films.
  • Medical conditions that can exclude guests include recent shoulder injury, pregnancy, and sinus or ear infections.
Know before you go
  • Book at least 48 hours ahead (HALO); a CHF 300 deposit secures the slot.
  • Cancel up to 48 hours before the jump for a deposit refund; bad-weather days are rescheduled or refunded.
  • Wear closed sports shoes, no hiking boots, and no hats, scarves or jewellery.
  • Spectators are welcome at the dropzone but cannot fly in the helicopter.
  • Skydive Interlaken offsets CHF 6.40 of carbon per jump through myclimate.
Cancellation & changes

HALO: free cancellation up to 48 hours before the jump; the deposit of CHF 300 is refunded. Bad weather: rescheduling or refund. Other jumps: Verify.

FAQs

How high is the HALO jump?

About 18,000 ft (5,500 m), giving about 80 seconds of freefall.

Is oxygen used?

Yes, from 8,000 ft during the helicopter flight. The mask comes off shortly before the jump, and the freefall is without a mask.

What are the weight limits?

On the HALO page: women up to 90 kg and BMI 27.5, men up to 100 kg and BMI 30, height up to 195 cm.

Can children jump?

From age 12, and under-18s need parental consent.
