Overview

Piz Gloria sits on the Schilthorn above Mürren in the Lauterbrunnen valley. A four-stage cable car from Stechelberg or from Mürren climbs to the summit, where the revolving restaurant gives views of the Eiger, Mönch, Jungfrau and over 200 other peaks.

The restaurant was the setting of On Her Majesty's Secret Service, and the James Bond Brunch is popular. At Birg, the Thrill Walk is a steel-and-glass walkway on the cliff face.

FAQs

How long does the trip take?

About 4–5 hours from Interlaken including 1–1.5 hours at the top.

Which departure point is better?

Stechelberg gives the full cable-car experience; Mürren is shorter and cheaper at CHF 91.40.

Do passes help?

Swiss Travel Pass and Half Fare Card halve the ticket. Berner Oberland Pass is free.

Is there a Bond experience?

Yes: the James Bond World exhibition and the Brunch at Piz Gloria.
