Overview

The Eiger Mountain Swoop is a world-first tandem mountain swoop. A 15-minute helicopter flight passes the Jungfraujoch and the start of the Aletsch Glacier and climbs to 16,500 ft (5,000 m), about 1,000 m above the Eiger summit. After a short freefall of 5–7 seconds the parachute opens, and you fly a controlled swoop of about 15 minutes along the Mittellegi ridge past the Mittellegihütte, with the Ischmeer glacier on one side and Grindelwald on the other, before landing in Grindelwald village after about 3,000 vertical metres.

It is sold only as a package for one or two people with a private helicopter, and there is one flight a day. The package includes the Ultimate Combo shot with a 360° camera.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Physical rating: high. Good general health required.
  • Men: maximum 100 kg (220 lbs) and BMI 30.0. Women: maximum 90 kg (198 lbs) and BMI 27.5. Maximum height 195 cm (6'3"). The operator may still refuse a jump on safety grounds even within these limits.
  • Minimum age 12: the child must decide independently and fit the harness. Under 18 needs a parent's consent. There is no upper age limit for guests in good health.
  • Medical points that can exclude guests: recent shoulder injury or dislocation, pregnancy, sinus or ear infection or inflammation.
Dress code & rules
  • No alcohol or drugs before the jump: the operator refuses guests it believes have consumed them.
  • No wrist or helmet cameras (such as GoPros): all filming is done by the instructor.
  • Closed flat shoes such as sneakers; hiking boots are not recommended. Avoid jewellery, scarves and hats.
  • A release of liability is signed before the jump: the operator states it carries no insurance for guests.
Know before you go
  • Book as a package: there is no solo price. A solo guest may not be paired at short notice.
  • The highlight is the parachute flight, not the freefall.
  • Spectators cannot fly in the helicopter.
  • Deposit CHF 300 per package; free cancellation up to 48 hours before the jump.
  • Book at least 48 hours ahead; pay the balance on site in cash or card.
  • Skydive Interlaken donates CHF 6.40 per jump to myclimate to offset carbon.
Cancellation & changes

Weather: the jump is rescheduled or the deposit is refunded. Cancellations: for the private HALO and Swoop packages, free up to 48 hours before the jump.

FAQs

How is it different from paragliding?

It starts from a helicopter at 5,000 m and flies faster, low along the East Ridge, rather than floating down from a hill.

How long is the freefall?

Only 5–10 seconds, because the swoop is the main part.

Does it include photos and video?

Yes, the Ultimate Combo shot with a 360° camera.

What are the weight and height limits?

Men up to 100 kg and BMI 30.0, women up to 90 kg and BMI 27.5, height up to 195 cm. The operator can still decline a jump on safety grounds.

What happens if the weather is bad?

The operator contacts guests the day before if in doubt, postpones on site for safety, and refunds the deposit if the jump cannot take place.

Can guests bring their own camera?

No wrist or helmet cameras are allowed: the instructor films, and photo and video packages are sold as add-ons.
