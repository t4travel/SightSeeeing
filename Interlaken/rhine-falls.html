Overview

The Rhine Falls near Schaffhausen drop 600,000 litres of water per second on average into a basin about 150 m wide. Viewpoints on both banks, Laufen Castle with the Känzeli platform right above the falls, and a boat to the rock platform give close-up views.

From Zurich the falls are a 45–60 minute train ride. From Interlaken it is a long day, about 2 hours to Zurich plus the transfer, so many itineraries combine it with Zurich sightseeing or Lindt Home of Chocolate.

The Zurich Tourism coach tour is a 4-hour tour departing Zurich at 08:30 daily April–October and on weekends November–March, with an optional boat at CHF 12 adult / CHF 8 child. The coach crosses into Germany near Schaffhausen: carry your passport (Indian passport holders need a valid Schengen visa). Train to the falls is free with a Swiss Travel Pass and half price with a Half Fare Card.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open year-round for the viewpoints. Boats run in the summer season, roughly April – October.

Hours: Viewpoints open at all times; boat times by operator: Verify.

Best time: Midday, with the sun on the spray.

Meeting point & getting there

Schloss Laufen am Rheinfall station (south bank) or Neuhausen Rheinfall station (north bank). Zurich HB to the falls takes 45–60 minutes.

FAQs

How long from Interlaken?

About 3 hours each way via Zurich.

Is a boat ride worth it?

Yes, it is the highlight; it costs about CHF 10–25.

Do I need my passport?

On the coach tour, yes: it passes briefly through Germany.

Is the Swiss Travel Pass useful?

Yes for the rail, free to the falls.
