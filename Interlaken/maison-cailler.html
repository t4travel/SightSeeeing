Overview

Maison Cailler in Broc is a working chocolate museum and factory in the Gruyère region. The visit tells the story of Cailler chocolate, ends with a free tasting and finishes in the shop. A station named Broc-Chocolaterie is a few minutes' walk away.

Workshops let guests make their own chocolates. Many groups combine Cailler with the Gruyères cheese dairy and castle.
