Overview

For guests who prefer a calmer experience, Skydive Interlaken sells scenic helicopter flights over Interlaken's lakes and valleys, along the glaciers and past the Jungfraujoch and the Aletsch Glacier, the largest glacier in Europe. The operator describes them as ideal for families and nature lovers. There is also a Lauterbrunnen version.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Physical rating: high. Good general health required.
  • Men: maximum 100 kg (220 lbs) and BMI 30.0. Women: maximum 90 kg (198 lbs) and BMI 27.5. Maximum height 195 cm (6'3"). The operator may still refuse a jump on safety grounds even within these limits.
  • Minimum age 12: the child must decide independently and fit the harness. Under 18 needs a parent's consent. There is no upper age limit for guests in good health.
  • Medical points that can exclude guests: recent shoulder injury or dislocation, pregnancy, sinus or ear infection or inflammation.
Dress code & rules
  • No alcohol or drugs before the jump: the operator refuses guests it believes have consumed them.
  • No wrist or helmet cameras (such as GoPros): all filming is done by the instructor.
  • Closed flat shoes such as sneakers; hiking boots are not recommended. Avoid jewellery, scarves and hats.
  • A release of liability is signed before the jump: the operator states it carries no insurance for guests.

FAQs

Is it suitable for children?

The operator says it is ideal for families: Verify age limits.

Do I jump?

No, it is a scenic flight only.

What do we see?

The lakes, glaciers and the Jungfraujoch.

What are the weight and height limits?

Men up to 100 kg and BMI 30.0, women up to 90 kg and BMI 27.5, height up to 195 cm. The operator can still decline a jump on safety grounds.

What happens if the weather is bad?

The operator contacts guests the day before if in doubt, postpones on site for safety, and refunds the deposit if the jump cannot take place.

Can guests bring their own camera?

No wrist or helmet cameras are allowed: the instructor films, and photo and video packages are sold as add-ons.
