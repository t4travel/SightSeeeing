About Interlaken

Interlaken lies on the Bödeli plain between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz, with the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau above it. It is the base for the Jungfrau region and for Switzerland's adventure sports, and the Holiday Region Interlaken covers Matten, Unterseen, Wilderswil, Bönigen, Iseltwald, Ringgenberg, Habkern, Beatenberg and the Lake Thun and Lake Brienz shores.

Getting there

  • By train: half-hourly connections from the main Swiss cities and airports. Bern 1 hr; Lucerne, Zurich and Basel 2 hrs; Geneva 3 hrs (Interlaken Tourism).
  • International rail links to Berlin, Hamburg (ICE), Munich, Amsterdam, Milan and Paris (TGV).
  • Nearest international airports: Zurich and Geneva. Interlaken is a stage on the Grand Train Tour of Switzerland and the Grand Tour of Switzerland.
  • Luzern–Interlaken Express by Zentralbahn: about 2 hours, scenic.

Getting around and passes

  • Swiss Travel Pass (3, 4, 6, 8 or 15 consecutive days; Flex within one month): unlimited rail, bus and boat, free entry to many museums, 50% off most mountain railways.
  • Swiss Half Fare Card: CHF 150 for one month, half price on most travel.
  • Interlaken Guest Card: free from your accommodation; free 2nd-class bus and train in the local zones plus discounts (2024 leaflet: Verify 2026).
  • Jungfrau Travel Pass: unlimited valley trains, Eiger Express, First and Männlichen gondolas; Jungfraujoch at a connecting fare (CHF 89 in peak season).
  • Berner Oberland Pass: free on many Bernese Oberland railways and boats.

Tips for Indian travellers

  • Jungfraujoch is at 3,454 m: advise slow walking, water and a day's flexibility for weather.
  • Peak Indian demand falls in the summer school holidays and the autumn festival weeks: reserve Jungfraujoch, Schilthorn and Titlis seats early (Verify).
  • Vegetarian choices are available at mountain restaurants; confirm Jain and no-onion-garlic requests in advance.
  • Carry passports on day trips that cross into Germany, such as Rhine Falls coach tours.
  • Switzerland Tourism India webinar (March 2024, Verify for 2026): Swiss Schengen visa applications can be made up to 6 months ahead through VFS Global, with processing of at least 13–15 working days from when documents reach the visa section.
