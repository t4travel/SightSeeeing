Overview

Fremantle Prison is Western Australia’s only UNESCO World Heritage-listed building. Guided tours cover the convict history, the modern prison, true crime stories, night-time torchlight visits and an underground Tunnels Tour.

The Convict Prison Tour price below is current to 30 June 2027. Prices for other tours are the last published table and are marked Verify.
