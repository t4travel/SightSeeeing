Overview

Rottnest Island is 19 km off the coast and has 63 bays and beaches and a large population of quokkas. Ferries run from Fremantle, Perth (Barrack Street) and Hillarys, and the island is explored on foot, by bike or by bus.

Official 2026–27 fares were not readable, so the prices below combine reseller listings (which include the island admission fee) and older operator tables: Verify every fare before quoting.

Best time: Early ferry to maximise island time.

Meeting point & getting there

Barrack Street Jetty (Pier 2/3), Perth; Fremantle B Shed; or Hillarys Boat Harbour, depending on the operator. A morning pick-up from Perth CBD hotels is offered on some tickets.

Know before you go
  • Quokkas are wild; do not touch or feed them.
  • Island admission fee may or may not be included: check the ticket.
  • Book bikes early in busy periods.

FAQs

How long is the ferry?

About 30 minutes from Fremantle, about 2 hours from Perth, depending on the service.

Is the island fee included?

On reseller tickets it was included; on operator fare tables it was listed separately at A$21.

Can I see quokkas?

Yes, quokkas are common but sightings are not guaranteed.

Do I need a car?

No. Cars are not used by visitors; hire bikes or take the bus.
