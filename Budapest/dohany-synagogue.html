Overview

The Dohány Street Synagogue seats 3,000 and is the largest synagogue in Europe. One ticket covers the whole complex: the synagogue, the Hungarian Jewish Museum, Heroes’ Temple, the Raoul Wallenberg Memorial Park, the Tree of Life memorial and the graveyard.

It is an active place of worship and a Holocaust memorial, so dress and behaviour rules are strict.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Closed on Saturdays and Jewish holidays. Hours can change at short notice for services and events.

Hours: Winter and summer hours differ and Friday closes earlier: Verify on the operator’s opening-hours page. The last entry is about an hour before closing.

Best time: Weekday morning. Summer queues at the ticket desk are long.

Meeting point & getting there

Dohány utca 2, 1074 Budapest, in the Jewish Quarter; use the visitor entrance shown on the ticket. Nearest stops: Deák Ferenc tér (Metros M1, M2, M3), Astoria (Trams 47, 49; Buses 7, 78).

FAQs

Is it open on Saturday?

No. It is closed on Saturdays and Jewish holidays.

Is there a dress code?

Yes. Cover shoulders and knees. Men may be asked to cover their heads.

Is a guide included?

A guided tour is an optional ticket tier. Check which tier is being sold.

Can we use the Budapest Card?

No. The complex is not covered by the card.
