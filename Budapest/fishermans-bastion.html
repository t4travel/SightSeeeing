Overview

A terraced lookout on Castle Hill beside Matthias Church, built with seven towers. The lower terraces are free at all hours. A ticket is needed only for the upper towers and terraces, and only during ticketed hours.

Outside those hours the whole structure is free, which makes sunrise and late evening the best times to go.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: No closed day. Ticket windows are free of charge on 15 March, 20 August, 23 October and between Christmas and New Year: Verify.

Hours: Ticket needed for the upper towers 09:00–19:00, extended to 21:00 from 1 June to 30 September. Free outside those hours. Verify on the operator’s site.

Best time: Sunrise, or just after the ticket window closes in the evening.

Meeting point & getting there

Szentháromság tér, Castle District, Budapest. Nearest access: Castle Hill funicular from Clark Ádám tér, bus 16, or a walk up from Buda Castle.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy walking, but the upper level is reached by stairs only.
  • No lift to the upper towers. Wheelchair users can use the free lower terraces.
Know before you go
  • The ticket is bought at the kiosk by the entrance, so no pre-booking is needed.
  • Midday is the busiest and most expensive time. Go early or late.
  • Tripods may be refused if the shoot looks commercial.
Cancellation & changes

Entry tickets are bought on site and carry no booking terms. Online tickets: Verify.

FAQs

Is the Bastion free?

The lower terraces always are. The upper towers are free outside ticketed hours.

Does the ticket include Matthias Church?

No. A separate or combined ticket applies: Verify.

When is the best light?

Sunrise for empty terraces, and the hour after the ticket window closes for sunset.

Can wheelchair users visit?

The lower terraces, yes. The upper towers have no lift.
