Overview

The official city pass, issued by BKK. It gives unlimited public transport and a set of free services, and discounts on more than 30 activities.

Free inclusions listed by BKK: Lukács Thermal Bath entry, a Danube cruise, the Zugliget chairlift, and heritage trams from May to October. Discounts of 10% to 25% apply at partners. The Dohány Synagogue complex is not covered.
