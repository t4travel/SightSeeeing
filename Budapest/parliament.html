Overview

The neo-Gothic seat of the National Assembly on the Pest bank of the Danube, one of the largest parliament buildings in the world. The visit covers the Grand Staircase, the Dome Hall with the Holy Crown of Hungary, and the Old Upper House Hall.

Entry is by timed slot, sold through the official seller jegymester.hu. Every visitor must show photo ID, and nationality decides the price: Indian-passport holders pay the non-EEA rate.

Your visit, step by step
Arrive at the Visitor Centre
Be there 20 minutes before your slot. The centre is on the river side of the building.
Security and ID check
Show your ticket and photo ID. The ID must match the nationality tier you bought.
Guided or audio visit
About 45 minutes through the staircase, Dome Hall and session hall.
Exit
You leave onto Kossuth Lajos tér, a short walk from the river.
Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Closed during parliamentary sessions and state events. National holidays (15 March, 20 August, 23 October) are usually closed to visitors: Verify.

Hours: Visit slots are published on jegymester.hu. English slots run roughly 10:00–15:00 and change by day: Verify.

Best time: Morning slots, when the building is quieter. Pair with an evening river cruise to see it lit.

Meeting point & getting there

Visitor Centre, on the river side of the Parliament, Kossuth Lajos tér 1–3, 1055 Budapest. Nearest stops: Kossuth Lajos tér (Metro M2) and Tram 2 along the Danube.

Know before you go
  • Buy only through the official seller (jegymester.hu) or a trusted DMC. Some resellers charge about twice the face value.
  • Tickets are date- and time-specific, so collect passport names before booking.
  • Same-day tickets are limited. Groups must pre-book.
Cancellation & changes

Official tickets are fixed to a date and time and are reported as non-changeable and non-refundable. Verify with the ticket supplier before booking.

FAQs

Which price do Indian guests pay?

The non-EEA rate, HUF 14,000 for an adult (student 6–24: HUF 7,000).

Is there a skip-the-line option?

No. The visit is by timed slot only, so the slot time is your entry time.

Do children need a ticket?

Under 6 enter free. Children 6–14 can use the family ticket (max 2 adults and 3 children): Verify.

Can we visit on 23 October?

Probably not. National holidays are normally closed to visitors, so confirm the date before selling.
