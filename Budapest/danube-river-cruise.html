Overview

A sightseeing cruise on the Danube that passes Parliament, Buda Castle, Chain Bridge and Margaret Island on some routes. Several private operators run near-identical boats from the piers by Elizabeth Bridge and Vigadó tér.

Prices differ by operator and by what is on board: a plain audio-guide cruise, a cruise with one welcome drink, or party boats with unlimited drinks.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: No closed day. Cruises can be cancelled for weather or high or low water.

Hours: Day, sunset and night departures; timetable by season: Verify with the operator.

Best time: Sunset or night, when Parliament and the bridges are lit.

Meeting point & getting there

Piers by Elizabeth Bridge on the Pest side, Jane Haining rakpart (Silverline Dock 11; Legenda at No. 7) and Vigadó tér. Nearest stops: Ferenciek tere (Metro M3) and Vigadó tér (Tram 2).

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy. Seated on board.
  • The Silverline day cruise is listed as not wheelchair accessible: Verify other boats.
Know before you go
  • Confirm which product is being sold: audio guide only, welcome drink, or unlimited drinks.
  • The audio guide is an app, so guests need a phone and earphones.
  • Party boats are louder and not suited to family groups.
  • Late arrival may mean rebooking at a surcharge.
Cancellation & changes

Weather cancellations follow the operator’s terms. Free-cancellation terms vary by operator: Verify.

FAQs

Which cruise is best for photos?

Sunset or night, for the lit Parliament and Chain Bridge.

Is the Budapest Card useful here?

The official city card includes one Danube cruise: Verify the current partner.

Are drinks included?

Only on products that say so. A plain cruise has none.

Is it suitable for elderly guests?

Yes. Boarding and seating are easy, but check access on the specific boat.
