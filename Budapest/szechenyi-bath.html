Overview

The largest thermal bath complex in Europe, in a 1913 neo-baroque building in City Park. The complex has 3 outdoor and 15 indoor pools, plus saunas and steam rooms.

Széchenyi has changed its rules. Since 1 August 2025 children under 14 are not admitted to the thermal pools, and the bath sells one daily ticket for all ages with no family ticket.

Your visit, step by step
Buy or scan your ticket
Scan your QR code or use a card-only machine at the entrance. Look for the priority sign on the fast-track lane.
Change
Use your locker or cabin to change. Bring your own towel and swimwear.
Pools and saunas
Move between indoor and outdoor pools. The ticket is valid all day for one entry.
Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily. Short maintenance closures are posted on the official site.

Hours: Hours differ by day and season: Verify at szechenyibath.hu/opening-hours.

Best time: Weekday morning, to avoid the queue and the higher weekend price.

Meeting point & getting there

Állatkerti körút 9–11, 1146 Budapest, in City Park. Nearest station: Széchenyi fürdő (Metro M1).

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy, with some stairs between areas and a lot of walking.

Dress code & rules
  • Children under 14 are not admitted to the thermal pools.
  • Swimwear is required. Towels and slippers are not rented on site: bring your own.
Know before you go
  • Do not buy from touts at the gate. Use the official site or a trusted DMC.
  • Students’ discount is not given at the on-site machines.
  • Weekend and holiday queues are long, so fast-track is worth it.
Cancellation & changes

Cancellation on official online tickets is reported as 72 hours’ notice. Verify before booking.

FAQs

Can children visit?

Not under 14. This rule applies since 1 August 2025.

What is the cheapest way in?

The Good Morning ticket, on weekdays Mon–Thu if you check in before 09:00: Verify.

Do we need to bring towels?

Yes. Towels are not rented and buying one on site is costly.

Is it included in the Budapest Card?

No, but the card gives a discount on Széchenyi. The free bath is Lukács: Verify.
