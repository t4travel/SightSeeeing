Overview

The 5th-century BC citadel above Athens, home to the Parthenon, the Erechtheion with its Caryatid porch, the Propylaea gateway and the Temple of Athena Nike. The ticket also covers the North and South Slopes.

Entry is by timed slot only. Your ticket is valid from 15 minutes before to 15 minutes after the slot, and free tickets must also be booked for a slot. Summer morning and late slots sell out first.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Typically closed 1 Jan, 25 Mar, 1 May, Orthodox Easter Sunday and 25–26 Dec. Verify the exact dates for your travel year.

Hours: Summer (1 Apr–31 Oct): 08:00–20:00, last entry about 19:30. Winter (1 Nov–31 Mar): 08:00–17:00, last entry about 16:30. The site can close temporarily in extreme heat.

Best time: First slot at 08:00 or the last slots of the day. Cruise-ship coach groups fill the site roughly 10:00–13:00.

Meeting point & getting there

Acropolis of Athens, Dionysiou Areopagitou, 117 42 Athens. Nearest station: Acropoli (Metro Line 2). Tickets are booked online, so go straight to the gate for your slot.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Moderate to strenuous. Uneven, polished marble with steep sections and almost no shade.
  • A lift for visitors with reduced mobility exists on the site. Booking rules: Verify with the site.
Dress code & rules
  • Wear flat shoes with grip, as the marble is slippery. High heels are not permitted — Verify.
  • Photo ID is needed for reduced and free tickets. Drones and large bags: Verify site rules.
Know before you go
  • Book the official ticket early in summer. Look-alike ticket sites exist, so buy from the official portal or a trusted reseller.
  • Bring water and a hat, as the plateau has no shade.
  • Free-admission days (such as 28 Oct) bring larger crowds and still need a booked slot.
Cancellation & changes

Official tickets are non-refundable. Date or time changes may be allowed up to 2 hours before the slot, up to twice — Verify. Reseller terms vary by product.

FAQs

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes. Entry is by timed slot only, and free tickets also need a slot.

Is the Acropolis Museum included?

No. It is a separate ticket, at €20 for an adult.

Is there a discount for Indian-passport holders?

There is no blanket discount. Indian passport holders pay the non-EU standard rate; non-EU visitors under 18 enter free with ID.

What is the best time of day?

The 08:00 slot, or late in the day in summer, to avoid heat and coach groups.
