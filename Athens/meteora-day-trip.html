Overview

A very long day trip to the monasteries of Meteora, about 360 km from Athens. The direct Athens–Kalambaka train is not running, so day trips go by coach.

Most visits enter two or three monasteries and see the rest from viewpoints. An overnight in Kalambaka gives a far more relaxed visit.

Know before you go
  • The direct train is not running; treat any train product with caution — Verify.
  • Confirm which monasteries are open on your date.
  • A one-night stay in Kalambaka is strongly advisable.

FAQs

Is the train an option?

Not at present; the direct Athens–Kalambaka service is suspended — Verify.

How many monasteries can we enter?

Usually two or three, depending on the weekday.

Is a day trip realistic?

Yes, but it is a 12 to 16 hour day; overnight is better.
