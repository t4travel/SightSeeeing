Overview

Greece’s main archaeological museum, with objects from the Neolithic to Late Antiquity. The Mycenaean gold, Cycladic figurines, the Antikythera Mechanism and large bronzes are the highlights.

It is rarely crowded, and weekday mornings are the quietest.

FAQs

What are the must-see objects?

The Mask of Agamemnon, the Antikythera Mechanism, the Artemision bronze and the Cycladic figurines.

Is it open on Tuesdays?

Only in the afternoon, from 13:00 to 20:00.

How does it differ from the Acropolis Museum?

It covers all of Greece over 7,000 years; the Acropolis Museum covers the hill alone.
