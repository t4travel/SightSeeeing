Overview

Open-top buses on an Athens line with stops at the Acropolis area, Temple of Zeus, Panathenaic Stadium and the National Archaeological Museum. Longer passes add the Piraeus and Beach-Riviera lines.

Piraeus and Riviera lines run April to October only.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: The Athens line runs all year except 17 Nov, 25 Dec and 1 Jan. Piraeus and Riviera lines run April to October — Verify.

Hours: The Athens line’s first departure is about 08:30 from Syntagma Square. The Piraeus line starts about 11:00 — Verify.

Best time: Take a full loop first, then hop off where you want to stay.

Meeting point & getting there

Athens line terminal near Syntagma Square (Stop 1). Check the bus company’s stop map for your hotel.

FAQs

Which pass is best?

The 48-hour all-lines pass suits guests who also want Piraeus and the coast.

Does it run in winter?

The Athens line does; Piraeus and Riviera lines do not.

Does it go to the Acropolis?

It stops near the Acropolis area; entry is a separate ticket.
