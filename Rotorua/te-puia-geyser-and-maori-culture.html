Overview

Te Puia is in Whakarewarewa, 2.5 km from central Rotorua. Its day experiences run from 9:00 to 17:00 and its night experiences from 17:00 to 22:00. The Te Rā Guided Experience is a 90-minute guided tour of Te Whakarewarewa Geothermal Valley with Pōhutu Geyser, the Kiwi Conservation Centre and the New Zealand Māori Arts and Crafts Institute. There is no self-guided option.

Evening options include the Te Pō hāngī dinner and cultural performance, and the Mārama Geyser Light Trail. Prices below are the operator’s rates on tourism and booking sites for the 2026–27 rate year: Verify.

FAQs

Can I walk around on my own?

No. Day access is through guided experiences.

How long is the Te Rā tour?

About 90 minutes.

What does the Te Pō evening include?

A hāngī buffet, a cultural performance and hot chocolate at the geyser.

Are children free?

Infants under 5 are free; ages 5–15 pay a child fare.
