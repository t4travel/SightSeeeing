Overview

Lapland Hotels SnowVillage in Lainio, Kittilä (Lainiotie 566) is rebuilt every winter and has an ice exhibition, snow suites, an ice restaurant and an ice bar. It opens on 25 December 2026 and runs to 4 April 2027, daily 10:00–22:00.

From Ylläs it is about 15 minutes by car (about 20 km from Ylläsjärvi, 30 km from Äkäslompolo). A bus connection from the Ylläs and Levi resorts runs daily during opening hours, and Lapland Safaris runs a 4.5-hour snowmobile safari to the SnowVillage in winter.

Lapland Safaris also lists a private 2.5-hour Excursion to the SnowVillage (€1,020 per group of 1–10; extra adult €102, child €76) and a 3.5-hour Dinner at the SnowVillage (€1,830 per group; extra adult €183, child €137). The 4.5-hour snowmobile safari is winter-season only (7 Jan–5 Apr 2027) with a €980 exclusive supplement. Tickets are sold on site.
