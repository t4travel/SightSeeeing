Overview

A 9th-century Moorish fortress on the highest hill in central Lisbon, taken by the first king of Portugal in 1147. Visitors walk the ramparts and towers and see peacocks in the gardens. The streets of Alfama below the castle are free to explore.

Sources give the 2026 adult ticket as €15 and €17, so confirm before quoting. The Lisboa Card gives free entry.

Meeting point & getting there

Rua de Santa Cruz do Castelo, 1100-129 Lisbon. Tram 28E stops at Largo das Portas do Sol, a 5-minute uphill walk; tram 12E stops at São Tomé.
