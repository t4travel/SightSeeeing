Overview

Porto is about 2 hours 40 minutes from Lisbon on the Alfa Pendular, with trains from Oriente and Santa Apolónia. A same-day return is possible but gives only a few hours in Porto. One travel guide calls a Lisbon–Porto day trip a waste of time: an overnight is better.

Fares from CP’s 2026 tariff: Alfa Pendular second class €35.70, first class €49.90. Intercidades second class is €28.05 and takes about 3 hours 10 minutes.

Meeting point & getting there

Lisboa Oriente or Santa Apolónia station. Alfa Pendular does not stop at Santa Apolónia on some Algarve routes: check the departure station.

Know before you go
  • A same-day return leaves only a few hours in Porto.
  • Book Alfa Pendular seats ahead: cheap seats sell out Friday evenings.
  • Indian-passport guests need a Schengen visa valid for Portugal: Verify the visa category and dates before selling.
