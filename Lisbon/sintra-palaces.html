Overview

Sintra, 30 minutes from Lisbon, has several monuments run by Parques de Sintra: Pena Palace, the Moorish Castle, Monserrate Palace and the Sintra National Palace. Quinta da Regaleira is run separately. Since 2024 the Pena Palace interior has needed a timed-entry slot; the park-only ticket has no time limit.

Sources disagree on 2026 prices. Most 2026 guides give about €20 for Pena Palace plus park, about €12 for the Moorish Castle and €12 for Monserrate, but one guide gives €14 for Pena and another €10 for the Castle and Monserrate. Quinta da Regaleira is listed at €10, €15 and €20. Confirm every figure at parquesdesintra.pt and regaleira.pt.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Monument closures differ and some close on set weekdays: Verify before selling. Pena time slots sell out before noon in summer.

Hours: Pena Palace opens at 09:00 with timed slots of 30 minutes. Other monument hours: Verify at parquesdesintra.pt.

Best time: Be at Pena at 09:00 or take a late slot. Weekdays are quieter.

Meeting point & getting there

Pena Palace, Estrada da Pena, Sintra. From Sintra station take bus 434 (Scotturb), a loop to the town centre, the Moorish Castle and Pena Palace. Trains run from Lisbon Rossio or Oriente to Sintra (return about €4.60).

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Moderate to strenuous. Steep paths, stairs and cobbles at Pena and the Moorish Castle.
  • Step-free access is limited: Verify with Parques de Sintra.
Know before you go
  • Do not try to see more than two or three monuments in a day.
  • Leave enough time to walk from the Pena gate to the palace.
  • Rescheduling on Parques de Sintra tickets is free within one year, per the official policy reported by a guide: Verify.
  • Indian-passport guests need a Schengen visa valid for Portugal: Verify the visa category and dates before selling.
Cancellation & changes

Parques de Sintra tickets can be rescheduled free of charge within a year, per one 2026 guide. Reseller terms: Verify.

FAQs

Is the Pena Palace ticket timed?

Yes for the interior. The park-only ticket is untimed.

Does the Lisboa Card cover Sintra?

No.

How many monuments in one day?

Two or three at most, typically Pena and the Moorish Castle, plus Regaleira.

Which price applies?

Check parquesdesintra.pt: 2026 guides disagree.
