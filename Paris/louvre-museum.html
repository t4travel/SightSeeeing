Overview

The world’s most-visited museum, home to the Mona Lisa, the Venus de Milo and the Winged Victory of Samothrace, in a former royal palace.

Entry is by timed slot and the museum is closed on Tuesdays.

Opening hours & closed days

Mon, Thu, Sat, Sun: 09:00–18:00. Wed & Fri: 09:00–21:00. Last entry 1 hour before closing.

Closed: Tuesdays, 1 January, 1 May and 25 December.

Best time: Wednesday or Friday evening, or first slot of the day.

Meeting point & getting there

Pyramid entrance, Rue de Rivoli, 75001 Paris. Nearest station: Palais Royal–Musée du Louvre (Metro 1, 7).

Know before you go
  • Your time slot is enforced, so arrive on time.
  • Tickets are sold on the official site up to 90 days ahead (Verify). Book only from the official site or a trusted reseller.
  • Do not buy from street vendors; the museum warns about fake queue-jump tickets.
  • Ticket limit is 6 per person per booking.

FAQs

Which rate applies to Indian passport holders?

The non-EEA rate of €32, as India is outside the European Economic Area.

Is the Louvre open every day?

No. It is closed on Tuesdays, and on 1 January, 1 May and 25 December.

How long do I need?

Plan 2 hours for the highlights and 3–4 hours for a fuller visit.

Can I buy tickets at the door?

Only when attendance is low and subject to availability, so book a slot online.
