Overview

The Catacombs hold the remains of about six million Parisians in former limestone quarries 20 m below the 14th arrondissement.

The Catacombs reopened on 8 April 2026 after a renovation. Entry is by timed slot only and slots sell out.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Physical rating: moderate. 131 steps down and about 112 up, with no lift.
  • Not suitable for people with claustrophobia or limited mobility.
Rules
  • Capacity is limited to 200 people per slot.
  • Security checks apply on entry.
Know before you go
  • No tickets are sold on site; book online in advance.
  • The Paris Museum Pass is not accepted here.
  • It is about 14°C inside, so bring a layer.
  • Book early: slots are released on a rolling window and sell out fast.

FAQs

Can I buy a ticket at the entrance?

No. All tickets, including free ones, must be booked online.

Is the audio guide included?

Yes, for Full, Reduced and Child 8–17 tickets.

Is it suitable for children?

Children 5+ may enter; it is not suitable for anyone with claustrophobia.

Does the Paris Museum Pass work here?

No.
