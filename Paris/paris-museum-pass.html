Know before you go
  • Reserve slots for the Louvre, Orsay, Sainte-Chapelle, Notre-Dame towers and Versailles.
  • Versailles needs a free Passport ticket.
  • The Catacombs and Eiffel Tower do not accept the pass.
  • The pass is non-refundable and non-exchangeable.
Cancellation & changes

The pass is non-refundable and non-exchangeable.

FAQs

When does the pass start?

At your first visit; it then runs 48, 96 or 144 hours.

Is the Eiffel Tower included?

No.

Do I still need to book?

Yes, at the most popular sites.

Is it worth it?

It pays off with several paid museums in a few days.
