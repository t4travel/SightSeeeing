Overview

The cathedral of St Andrew stands at the top of 57 steps in Piazza Duomo. One ticket covers the Cloister of Paradise, the Basilica of the Crucifix with the Diocesan Museum, and the crypt of St Andrew.

The cloister was built in 1266–68 as a burial ground for Amalfi’s nobles and has interlaced Arab-style arches.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily. Some sources show the Diocesan Museum closed on Mondays; Verify.

Hours: Hours change by season (about 09:00 to 18:45 in spring and summer, shorter in winter). Verify on the day.

Best time: Right at opening, before cruise and coach groups arrive.

Meeting point & getting there

Piazza Duomo, 84011 Amalfi. From Positano by ferry about 20 minutes (seasonal) or by SITA bus; Verify.

Dress code & rules
  • Shoulders and knees must be covered.
  • Quiet during services; photography limits in the church: Verify.
Know before you go
  • Sold from: Positano, Amalfi, Sorrento and Salerno as a coast stop.
  • Weddings can close parts of the cathedral briefly.
  • Entry to the nave during services is free; the cloister needs the ticket.

FAQs

Is the cathedral free?

The church is free to enter outside paid hours, but the cloister, crypt and museum need the €3 ticket.

How long do I need?

Plan 45 to 60 minutes including the steps.

Is there a dress code?

Yes. Cover shoulders and knees.
