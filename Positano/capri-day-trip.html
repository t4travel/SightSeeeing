Overview

Capri is reached by hydrofoil from Sorrento (about 20–25 minutes), Naples, or seasonally from Amalfi and Positano (about 50–60 minutes). Some operators run boat tours from Sorrento, Positano and Amalfi.

The tour boat does not enter the Blue Grotto. Visitors transfer to a small rowboat, and the cave entry is paid separately in cash at the grotto.

FAQs

Is the Blue Grotto included?

Usually not. It is bought separately at the cave.

From where is Capri closest?

Sorrento, about 20–25 minutes by hydrofoil.

What if the grotto is closed?

Operators offer an island tour or a refund per their terms.
