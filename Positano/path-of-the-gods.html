Overview

A high trail along the cliffs above Praiano and Positano with wide views of the coast and Capri. The usual route runs from Bomerano (Agerola) to Nocelle.

The route has no ticket. Guided and transfer-inclusive versions are sold by operators.

Finish at Nocelle
Onward by bus or steps down to Positano (long, steep stairs).
Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open-air trail.

Hours: Walk in daylight. Avoid after heavy rain.

Best time: Early morning in warm months.

Meeting point & getting there

Walk finishes at Nocelle above Positano, then the Nocelle–Positano bus or about 1,500 steps down. Starts at Bomerano (Agerola); transfer from Positano by bus or taxi (Verify).

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Strenuous for some guests: uneven stone and steps, no shade in places.
  • Not suitable for wheelchairs or strollers.
Dress code & rules
  • Wear hiking shoes.
Know before you go
  • Take water and sun protection.
  • Descending from Nocelle to Positano is a long staircase; many guests take the bus.
  • Sold from: Amalfi, Positano and Sorrento (transfers).

FAQs

Is the hike free?

Yes, the trail has no entry fee.

How hard is it?

Moderate, with uneven ground; suited to active guests.

Do we need a guide?

No, but a guide helps with transfers and route.
