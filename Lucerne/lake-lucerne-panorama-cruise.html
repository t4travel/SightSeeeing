Overview

The SGV one-hour cruise sails across the Lucerne lake basin with views of the city, the lake and Pilatus and Rigi. From 1 August to 18 October 2026 it runs on the Saphir H₂, described by the operator as Switzerland's first hydrogen-powered passenger vessel, with a convertible roof and a free audio tour.

Earlier in 2026 another motor vessel ran the cruise at a reduced price without an audio guide. The cruise has no class distinction.
