Overview

The vintage wooden funicular from Stans (1893) climbs to Kälti, where the CabriO cable car with an open upper deck takes over for the last stretch. The ride is about 25 minutes each way.

At the top are a revolving restaurant, viewing terraces with views of ten Swiss lakes and a marmot park.
