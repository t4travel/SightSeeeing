Overview

The Jungfraujoch is the highest railway station in Europe, reached from Interlaken Ost by train and cable car. At the top are the Sphinx observation platform over the UNESCO Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch area, the Ice Palace with its ice sculptures, and the Aletsch Glacier, Europe's longest.

Two routes lead up. Guests can take the train via Grindelwald or Lauterbrunnen and Kleine Scheidegg, or ride the Eiger Express gondola from Grindelwald Terminal to Eigergletscher, about 15 minutes, and change to the Jungfrau Railway. Visibility depends on the day, so check the live webcams before departure.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Open daily, but trains and cable cars can be suspended in strong wind or storms.

Hours: First departures from Interlaken Ost are about 07:35; last return trains leave the summit in the late afternoon. Exact timetable: Verify on the day.

Best time: Early morning departures, before cloud builds, for the clearest views.

Meeting point & getting there

Lucerne railway station for the Express; Interlaken Ost is the start of the Jungfrau railways. A guided bus and cogwheel day tour from Lucerne is listed on the Lucerne portal: Verify the price.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Physical rating: moderate. The altitude can cause headaches or breathlessness; walk slowly and drink water.
  • Trains and cabins are accessible; Ice Palace has slopes and steps. Wheelchair routes: Verify with Jungfrau Railways.
  • Not advised for guests with heart or lung conditions without medical advice.
Know before you go
  • A same-day return from Lucerne takes about 3.5 hours each way: an overnight in Interlaken is strongly advised.
  • Check live webcams before departure.
  • A seat reservation is needed on peak days: Verify.
  • Swiss Travel Pass gives 25% off the top section only.
Cancellation & changes

Ticket change and refund rules: Verify with the seller. Rail operators normally refund when services are suspended for weather.

FAQs

How much time does the full day take?

About 6–8 hours from Interlaken including 3 hours at the top.

Is the ticket cheaper with a pass?

Yes: the Half Fare Card halves it, the Swiss Travel Pass gives 25% off the top section, and the Jungfrau Travel Pass sets a connecting fare of CHF 89 in peak season.

Which route is best?

The Eiger Express from Grindelwald Terminal is faster; the train via Kleine Scheidegg is more scenic.

What if the weather is bad?

Move the day to a clear forecast or add buffer days to a trip; the summit can be in cloud.
