Overview

From 1 April 2026 the Lucerne Travel Pass replaces the Tell-Pass for Central Switzerland. It offers free travel on boats on Lakes Lucerne and Uri, city buses around Lucerne, and mountain routes including Rigi, Pilatus, Stanserhorn, Bürgenstock and the Hammetschwand Lift, Seelisberg, Klewenalp and Titlis including the Ice Flyer.

The 2-day pass and the summer/winter price split no longer apply.
