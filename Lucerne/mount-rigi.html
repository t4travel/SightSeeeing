Overview

Rigi rises above Lake Lucerne and is reached by cogwheel trains from Vitznau (about 30 minutes) and Arth-Goldau (about 45 minutes), and by a cable car from Weggis. The Vitznau railway opened in 1871 as Europe's first mountain railway.

The Rigi Panorama path and many soft walking trails run around the summit. A common loop is boat Lucerne – Vitznau, cogwheel to Rigi Kulm, then down via Arth-Goldau and train back to Lucerne.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Operates every day of the year; a short Arth-Goldau cogwheel closure is listed for April 2027.

Hours: Vitznau first departures around 06:00, last about 22:00; Goldau about 08:00 to 19:00 (guide): Verify.

Best time: Morning departure for clear views.

Meeting point & getting there

Lucerne pier 1 (boat to Vitznau) or Lucerne station (train to Arth-Goldau). Lucerne to Rigi Kulm takes 1h15–2h depending on the route.
