Overview

Rigi rises above Lake Lucerne and is reached by cogwheel trains from Vitznau (about 30 minutes) and Arth-Goldau (about 45 minutes), and by a cable car from Weggis to Rigi Kaltbad and from Kräbel to Rigi Scheidegg. The Vitznau Rigi Railway opened in 1871 as Europe's first mountain railway. The mountain is 100% included in the Swiss Travel Pass.

Beyond the classic boat-and-train loop, Rigi offers a mineral bath and spa by Mario Botta at Rigi Kaltbad, steam trains, two cheese dairies, cave dinners, group lunches at Lok 7 and the LiRi light festival at Rigi Kaltbad from 5 to 21 February 2027.

Cancellation & changes

Group cancellations of publicly scheduled experiences are free up to 4 weeks ahead; later cancellations follow the operator's terms (2027 manual, Verify).

FAQs

Is Rigi free with the Swiss Travel Pass?

Yes, 100% including the cogwheel trains and cable cars; the Half Fare Card gives 50%.

What does the 2027 day pass cost?

CHF 84 for adults; children travel free with an adult.

Are groups cheaper?

Yes: groups of 10 or more paying adults get a group rate; ask for a quote.

Are vegetarian group meals available?

Yes, the group menus include vegetarian dishes such as a vegetable-and-curry menu at Lok 7.
