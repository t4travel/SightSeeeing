Overview

The Lion Monument, carved into a rock face in the Old Town, is free to see. The Glacier Garden next door is a geological open-air museum over 20 million years old, with glacial potholes, a rock world and a mirror labyrinth.

The nearby Bourbaki Panorama is a 112 × 10 m circular painting showing the internment of 87,000 French soldiers who fled to Switzerland in winter 1871.

Meeting point & getting there

The Lion Monument is a 10-minute walk from Lucerne station through the Old Town; the Glacier Garden and Bourbaki Panorama are beside it.
