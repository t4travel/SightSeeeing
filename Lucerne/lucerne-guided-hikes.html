Overview

Lucerne Tourism offers three guided hikes: the Rigi Panorama Trail from Rigi Kaltbad via the Felsenweg and Rigi First (easy, 1.5–2 hours, with the boat and cogwheel train up and a cable car down to Weggis); the Bürgenstock Felsenweg circular hike (easy, 2 hours, with an optional Hammetschwand Lift ride); and a round trip over the Sonnenberg (medium, 3 hours) with a funicular start from Gütsch.

Guides are employees of Lucerne Tourism, trained annually, and replacements are arranged if a guide drops out. Meals are not included.
