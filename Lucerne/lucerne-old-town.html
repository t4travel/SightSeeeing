Overview

Lucerne's medieval centre sits on the River Reuss where it leaves Lake Lucerne. The roofed Chapel Bridge and its stone Water Tower are the city's emblem, with historic churches such as the Jesuit Church and modern architecture like the KKL culture and congress centre beside them.

Walking the Old Town is free. Lucerne Tourism runs a regular 1.5-hour guided city tour and private guides; there is also an official audio-tour app, with downloads for offline use.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: None: outdoor sights are open at all times.

Hours: Guided tours depart by arrangement; the Tourist Information desk is at Bahnhofstrasse 3 (opening hours: Verify).

Best time: Early morning, before 09:00, for the best light and fewest people.

Meeting point & getting there

Lucerne Tourist Information, Bahnhofstrasse 3, 6002 Lucerne, next to the railway station; Chapel Bridge is about 5 minutes' walk.

Know before you go
  • Free and best done in the morning; add the Lion Monument and Glacier Garden for a full half day.

Cancellation & changes

Guided tours: no charge when cancelled 31 days or more before; CHF 90 between 1 and 30 days before; 100% within 24 hours or for a no-show (Lucerne Tourism sheet, Verify).
