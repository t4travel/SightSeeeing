Overview

Rudolf's Christmas, the lakeside market with a view of Lake Lucerne, runs from 20 November to 23 December 2026. The Lucerne Christmas Market on Franziskanerplatz runs 3–21 December 2026 in the Old Town, with stalls from 11:00 to 20:00 and food stalls to about 21:00 (guide and the 2023 sheet), closed on Christmas Day.

Also on the list: a craft market on 5–21 December, the Schweizerhof hotel Christmas market, the Venite international Christmas forum, Ice Magic skating, a Christmas train with live storytelling, a life-size nativity and SGV Christmas boat trips.
