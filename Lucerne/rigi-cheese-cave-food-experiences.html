Overview

Alp First is a newly built cheese dairy 20 minutes' walk from Rigi Kaltbad, opened in early summer 2025. Alp Chäserenholz is an alpine hut where the farmer hosts cheese tastings, butter and cheese making, fondue, raclette and an alpine barbecue.

In the Grüebisbalm cave, a cave said to be 100,000 years old, the owner and chef hosts dinners. Group lunches are served at Lok 7 and the Bahnhöfli restaurant, including vegetarian menus.
