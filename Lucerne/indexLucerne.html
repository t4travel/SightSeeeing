About Lucerne

Lucerne sits where Lake Lucerne meets the River Reuss, with the medieval Chapel Bridge and Water Tower, the Lion Monument and the KKL culture centre. The boat piers are right outside the railway station, and Rigi, Pilatus, Titlis, Bürgenstock and Stanserhorn are all reached from here, which makes it the hub of Central Switzerland.

Getting around and passes

  • Swiss Travel Pass (3, 4, 6, 8 or 15 consecutive days; Flex within one month): unlimited rail, bus and boat, free entry to many museums, 50% off most mountain railways.
  • Swiss Half Fare Card: CHF 150 for one month, half price on most travel.
  • Lucerne Travel Pass (from 1 April 2026, replacing the Tell-Pass): free boats on Lakes Lucerne and Uri, city buses and 40+ mountain routes including Rigi, Pilatus, Stanserhorn, Bürgenstock and Titlis.
  • Lucerne Visitor Card: free from your hotel; zone 10 transport and museum discounts.
  • Lucerne Museum Card: eight museums for two days from CHF 39.

Tips for Indian travellers

  • An Indian restaurant (Kanchi, Zürichstrasse 4) is on Lucerne Tourism's restaurant list; the Rigi group menus include a vegetable curry with rice (see the Rigi pages).
  • Lucerne's city-tour languages do not list Hindi: request an English guide and an interpreter, or Verify with Lucerne Tourism.
  • The Mount Rigi sales manual lists group meals for vegetarians: a vegetarian curry is offered at Lok 7.
  • Switzerland Tourism India webinar (March 2024, Verify for 2026): Swiss Schengen visa applications can be made up to 6 months ahead through VFS Global, with processing of at least 13–15 working days from when documents reach the visa section.
