Overview

Saariselkä is a fell resort in Finnish Lapland, about 250 km north of the Arctic Circle, with the nearest airport at Ivalo. Lapland Safaris’ 2026–27 winter safaris run from 1 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, split into a Christmas and New Year season (1 December–6 January) and a winter season (7 January–4 April).

Aurora season in the area runs roughly late August to April. Operators provide thermal clothing for most safaris, but safaris, especially snowmobile, husky and reindeer, can be physically demanding.

Know before you go
  • All Lapland Safaris safaris require at least 2 adults.
  • Children under 2 are not suitable for snowmobile and husky safaris.
  • Snowmobile excess is up to €800 per snowmobile; solo driving about €50 per person extra.

FAQs

What is an exclusive departure?

A private group departure with a per-group supplement.

Are clothing items provided?

Thermal overalls, boots and mittens are normally provided.

Are safaris guaranteed?

Departures are guaranteed but can change with weather.

Who can I contact?

The Lapland Safaris Saariselkä office.
