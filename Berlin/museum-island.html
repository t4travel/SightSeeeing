Overview

Museum Island holds the Altes Museum, Neues Museum, Alte Nationalgalerie, Bode-Museum and the Pergamon Museum. The Neues Museum, home of the Nefertiti bust, is the most visited.

The Pergamon Museum's main building is closed for renovation and is reported to reopen on 4 June 2027 (Verify). In the meantime the temporary exhibition 'Pergamon Museum. Das Panorama' at Am Kupfergraben 2 is open.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: the state museums are closed on Mondays. The Pergamon main building is closed until its reopening (reported 4 June 2027: Verify).

Hours: usually Tuesday to Sunday from 10:00 to 18:00, with a later close on some days: Verify per museum.

Best time: do the Neues Museum first thing, as it has the biggest queues.

Meeting point & getting there

James-Simon-Galerie (visitor centre), Bodestraße 1–3, 10178 Berlin. Pergamon Panorama: Am Kupfergraben 2. Nearest stations: Museumsinsel (U5) and Hackescher Markt (S-Bahn).

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy. The museums are step-free, but a full day involves a lot of walking.
  • The Panorama site is wheelchair accessible, according to a 2026 guide: Verify.
Know before you go
  • Time slots are advised for the Neues Museum, the Alte Nationalgalerie and the Panorama.
  • Day tickets bought online are normally non-refundable once used: Verify.
  • Pick two or three museums rather than all five.
Cancellation & changes

Free cancellation windows of 24 to 48 hours apply on some reseller tickets. Verify with the supplier. Activated day passes are typically non-refundable.

FAQs

Is the Pergamon Altar open?

No. The main Pergamon building is closed for renovation. The Panorama is open instead.

Which ticket is best?

A day ticket pays off from the second museum.

Is there a pass that covers it?

Yes. The Museum Pass Berlin covers the open Museum Island museums over three days: Verify the price.

Are children charged?

Under 18 enter free.
