Overview

The glass dome and roof terrace sit on top of the building where the German parliament meets. Entry is free, but every visitor must register in advance and show ID at the entrance.

The Bundestag's own registration is the official route. Listings that charge money are guided tours that include the dome.

Your visit, step by step
Register online
Book through the Bundestag's visitor portal with every guest's name and date of birth. Same-day confirmations are possible at the Visitors' Service centre near the Reichstag if places remain.
Arrive early
Come well before your slot, with ID for every guest.
ID and security check
Identity is checked at registration and again at the visitor entrance, followed by a security screening.
Lift and ramp
Take the lift to the roof terrace, then walk the spiral ramp inside the dome.
Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: the dome is closed 19 to 30 October 2026. For other closures call the Visitors' Service on +49 30 227-32083 (Mon 09:00–15:00, Tue–Thu 09:00–16:00, Fri 09:00–13:30).

Hours: daily 08:00 to 24:00, last admission 21:45, as published by third-party guides. Verify on bundestag.de.

Best time: a sunset slot for the city lights, or the first slots of the day for fewer visitors.

Meeting point & getting there

Reichstag building, Platz der Republik 1, 11011 Berlin. Nearest stations: Bundestag (U5) and Brandenburger Tor (S1, S2, S25; U5). Berlin Hauptbahnhof is about a 15-minute walk.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy. Lift to the roof and a gently sloping spiral ramp, with no stairs.
  • Details for wheelchair users: Verify with the Visitors' Service.
Dress code & rules
  • Proof of identity is needed to register and at the entrance, for every visitor. Carry your passport.
  • Security screening applies. Bag size limits: Verify.
Know before you go
  • Registration is compulsory, with no walk-in without a confirmation.
  • Do not buy 'Reichstag tickets' from third parties. The visit itself is free.
  • Group registration lead time and size limits: Verify with the Visitors' Service. Book as soon as dates are firm.
  • The dome can also close for security events at short notice.
Cancellation & changes

The visit is free of charge. If dates change, register again for the new date through the Bundestag portal. Rules on transfers: Verify.

FAQs

Is the dome free?

Yes. The visit is free, with advance registration.

Can I just turn up?

Only through the Visitors' Service centre, if places remain. The confirmation is issued at least two hours before the visit.

Is the dome open in October 2026?

It is closed from 19 to 30 October 2026.

What ID do I need?

Registration asks for last name, first name and date of birth. ID is checked at registration and at the entrance. Carry your passport.
