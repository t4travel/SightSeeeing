Overview

Sachsenhausen was a Nazi concentration camp north of Berlin, now a memorial and museum. Entry to the memorial and museums is free.

Many visitors join a guided tour from Berlin, which covers the train both ways. Allow 2.5 to 3.5 hours on site.

Your visit, step by step
Travel from Berlin
Take the S1 to Oranienburg, about 45 minutes from central Berlin: Verify the route on the day.
Arrive at the memorial
Walk from Oranienburg station or take a bus. The gate is at Tower A.
Tour or self-guided visit
Join a guided tour of about 3 hours or use the audio guide.
Return to Berlin
Allow 2.5 to 3.5 hours on site, plus the journey.
Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: museum buildings are reported closed on Mondays. Verify on the memorial's site.

Hours: reported 08:30 to 18:00 from April to September and 08:30 to 16:30 from October to March: Verify.

Best time: arrive in the morning to leave time for the grounds.

Meeting point & getting there

Straße der Nationen 22, 16515 Oranienburg. Guided tours usually start in central Berlin. Nearest station: Oranienburg (S1), then a walk or bus.

Dress code & rules
  • The site is a memorial and a graveyard. Visitors are asked to behave respectfully.
  • Photography rules: Verify on site.
Know before you go
  • Public tours are recommended for visitors aged 14 and over. Some operators accept 13 and over.
  • Daylight is short in winter, so plan the day.
  • Bring food and drink, as options at the site are limited.
  • Tours need a minimum number of guests, around four: Verify.
Cancellation & changes

Cancellation terms for guided tours: Verify with the operator. Some listings allow no changes.

FAQs

Is it free?

Entry to the memorial and museums is free. Tours add a cost.

What age is suitable?

The memorial recommends public tours from age 14.

How long does it take?

Allow 5 to 6 hours with the train, and 2.5 to 3.5 hours on site.

Do we need a guide?

No, but a guide or the audio guide gives the history.
