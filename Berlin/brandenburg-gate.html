Overview

The neoclassical gate on Pariser Platz, topped by the Quadriga sculpture, was completed in 1791. Through the Cold War it stood in the border zone, and today it is the city's main meeting point and photo stop.

There is no ticket. Most guided walks include the gate, and the Reichstag and the Holocaust Memorial are a short walk away.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: none. The square is open every day, around the clock.

Hours: public square, always accessible. Guided-tour start times depend on the operator: Verify.

Best time: early morning or after dark for photos with fewer people. Midday is the busiest.

Meeting point & getting there

Pariser Platz, 10117 Berlin. Nearest station: Brandenburger Tor (S-Bahn S1, S2, S25; U5).

FAQs

Do I need a ticket?

No. The gate stands on a public square and is free at all hours.

Can groups go inside the gate?

No. It is an open monument, so there is no interior visit.

What else is close by?

The Reichstag and the Holocaust Memorial are both a short walk away.

When are photos easiest?

Early morning or after dark, when the square is quieter.
