Overview

Checkpoint Charlie was the border crossing for foreigners, diplomats and Allied forces between East and West Berlin. The guard house on the corner is a replica, and the open-air site on the street is free.

The Wall Museum next door, a private museum open since the early 1960s, tells the story of the Wall and of escapes, with devices people used to cross.

FAQs

Is Checkpoint Charlie free?

The street site is free. The Wall Museum charges an entry fee.

Is the guard house original?

No. It is a replica.

Does the Museum Pass Berlin cover the Wall Museum?

No. It is a private museum.

Where can guests see the real Wall?

At the Berlin Wall Memorial on Bernauer Straße and at the East Side Gallery.
