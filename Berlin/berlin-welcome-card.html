Overview

Two products cover different needs. The Museum Pass Berlin gives free entry to more than 30 museums on three consecutive days, with no transport. The Berlin WelcomeCard gives public transport and discounts at many attractions.

The WelcomeCard also comes in a Museum Island version that adds one entry a day to the Museum Island museums. ABC zone versions include Potsdam and the airport.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: the pass follows each venue. State museums close on Mondays.

Validity: the Museum Pass runs three consecutive days from first use. WelcomeCard durations run from 48 hours to 6 days.

Best time: buy the pass for days when you will visit several museums.

Know before you go
  • The Museum Pass has no transport, and the WelcomeCard has no free museum entry unless it is the Museum Island version.
  • Private museums such as the DDR Museum are not covered by the Museum Pass.
  • Passes bought through transport-operator channels may not import into the app: Verify.
  • Do the sums: three paid museum visits repay the Museum Pass.

No. It is a private museum.
