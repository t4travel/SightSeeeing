Overview

Peter Eisenman's field of 2,711 concrete stelae stands beside the Brandenburg Gate. Beneath it the Information Centre documents the persecution and murder of Europe's Jews, including a room where names and short biographies are read aloud.

The field is free and always open. The Information Centre is free too, with set hours.

Your visit, step by step
The Field of Stelae
Walk in from any side at any hour. Give it a few quiet minutes first.
Information Centre
Take the entrance at the south-east corner of the field. Bags and backpacks go in the free cloakroom.
Leave time to reflect
Allow 45 to 90 minutes for the centre, and more if you listen to the names.
Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: the Information Centre is closed on Mondays. The field is open daily, all day.

Hours: Field of Stelae open 24 hours. Information Centre Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00, last admission 17:15 (Verify before the visit).

Best time: morning, when it is quiet, and a weekday for the centre.

Meeting point & getting there

Cora-Berliner-Straße 1, 10117 Berlin, just south of the Brandenburg Gate. Nearest stations: Brandenburger Tor (S1, S2, S25; U5) and Potsdamer Platz.

Accessibility & physical rating
  • Easy for the walking route, but the ground between the stelae is uneven cobblestone.
  • The Information Centre has lift access with staff support, according to the foundation.
Dress code & rules
  • Backpacks and bags must go in the free cloakroom at the Information Centre.
  • The site is a memorial. Visitors are asked to behave respectfully.
Know before you go
  • The content is emotionally heavy. Use discretion with young children.
  • The Information Centre has security checks and set hours.
  • Public tours run on selected dates: Verify at stiftung-denkmal.de.
Cancellation & changes

There is no ticket. Cancellation terms for guided tours: Verify with the operator.

FAQs

Is it free?

Yes. The field and the Information Centre are both free.

Is it open on Mondays?

The field is. The Information Centre is closed on Mondays.

How long does a visit take?

Allow about 45 to 90 minutes for the Information Centre, and at least 75 to 90 minutes with the field.

Are guided tours available?

The foundation lists free public tours on selected dates. Operators also sell guided walks: Verify.
