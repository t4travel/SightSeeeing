Overview

Several routes run on the Spree and the Landwehr Canal, from a one-hour city cruise to a 3.25-hour bridge tour. The boats pass the government district, Museum Island and Berlin Cathedral.

A two-hour catamaran route from Jannowitzbrücke passes the East Side Gallery and the Oberbaum Bridge.

Meeting point & getting there

Pier depends on the route. The 2 hr 15 min cruise leaves from Reichstagufer at Friedrichstraße. The catamaran leaves from Jannowitzbrücke, Rolandufer 4. Other piers are used: Verify at booking.
