Overview

Zurich Tourism's public Old Town Walking Tour starts at the Tourist Information in the main station and lasts about two hours. It covers the Altstadt lanes on the Limmat, the Grossmünster, the Lindenhof and guild houses. Your destination file asks that the Chagall stained-glass windows of the Fraumünster are included in every Zurich tour, and the church charges its own entry fee of CHF 5.

The Grossmünster's Karlsturm tower, a climb with a view over the Old Town, is listed at about CHF 5 for adults (Verify).
