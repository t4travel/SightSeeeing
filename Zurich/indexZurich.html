About Zurich

Zurich is Switzerland's largest city and the main arrival airport for Indian travellers. Its compact Old Town on the Limmat, Lake Zurich, the Chagall windows of the Fraumünster, Bahnhofstrasse shopping and the Uetliberg lookout can be combined with day trips to Lucerne, Rigi, Titlis, the Rhine Falls and Bern.

Airport and arrival

  • Zurich Airport has its own railway station: train to the main station 10 min (CHF 7), tram about 40 min (CHF 7), taxi about 20 min (about CHF 60) — 2024 webinar figures, Verify.
  • The Zurich Card covers the airport transfer in the airport zone: 24 hrs CHF 29, 72 hrs CHF 56 (adult), children 6–15 CHF 19 and CHF 37; prices change on 13 December 2026: Verify.
  • Zurich Tourism is based in the main station (Im Hauptbahnhof).

Getting around and passes

  • Swiss Travel Pass (3, 4, 6, 8 or 15 consecutive days; Flex within one month): unlimited rail, bus and boat, free entry to many museums, 50% off most mountain railways.
  • Swiss Half Fare Card: CHF 150 for one month, half price on most travel.
  • Zurich Card: free public transport in zones 110 and surrounding zones, Limmat river cruiser, short lake cruises, Uetliberg, and free or discounted entry to about 50 museums; 50% off the Old Town Walking Tour; 10% off shops.
  • Most museums close on Mondays.
