Overview

Milford Sound (Piopiotahi) is a fiord in Fiordland National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage area. Cruises run its full length past Mitre Peak and Stirling Falls, with chances to see fur seals, bottlenose dolphins and the rare Fiordland crested penguin.

Prices below are from a New Zealand booking site for 2026 dates and change a lot by date. Two operators are shown: Southern Discoveries and RealNZ. Mitre Peak Cruises runs a smaller 2-hour cruise: its price was not found. Verify every price.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: Verify; weather-dependent

Hours: Cruises run through the day; the buffet lunch is on selected sailings. Allow time to drive: about 5 hours from Queenstown or 3 hours from Te Anau. Verify.

Best time: Late morning for fewer coaches; calm days give the best views.

Meeting point & getting there

Milford Sound Visitor Terminal, State Highway 94, Milford Sound. Self-drive guests should arrive at least one hour before departure.
