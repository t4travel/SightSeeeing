Overview

ABBA The Museum on Djurgården is an interactive experience built around the group’s music, outfits and performances, where visitors can sing, dance and record themselves.

Tickets are sold online for a fixed entry time and prices change by date and time of day. Book ahead, especially in summer.

Opening hours & closed days

Closed days: 25 Dec (Christmas Day); short hours 24 Dec

Hours: September–23 December: daily 10:00–18:00. Summer (about 1 June–mid August): roughly 09:00–20:00 daily. 24 December short hours, 25 December closed (official site; Verify for your date).

Best time: At opening or late afternoon; weekday mornings are calmest.

Meeting point & getting there

Djurgårdsvägen 68, 115 21 Stockholm. Tram 7, bus 67 (Liljevalchs/Gröna Lund) or the Djurgården ferry (Allmänna Gränd).
